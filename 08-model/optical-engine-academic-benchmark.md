# Academic optical-engine benchmark

**Owner:** Nur Alpys
**Status:** Evidence benchmark; not a product ranking
**As of:** 2026-08-10

## Purpose and comparability rule

This table extracts the strongest quantitative boundaries from the retained academic packet. A device result, a modeled transmitter result and a packaged engine result are different evidence classes. No row should be used as a direct commercial ranking unless the lane rate, modulation, optical reach, laser boundary, thermal boundary, FEC/BER method, packaging and power accounting are matched.

## What the benchmark says about the active thesis

1. The best direct PIC evidence and the best high-power-laser evidence come from different architectures and suppliers. A high-output laser result does not establish PIC or package leadership, and a dense PIC result does not establish a qualified optical engine.
2. PAP-025 is the closest retained record to a 200G/lane transmitter-engine boundary, but it reports only a representative channel's RF performance and eight-channel optical output. It should inform the 200G engine feasibility gate, not the production-volume or profit-pool gate.
3. PAP-019 and PAP-022 make the external-laser decision a real engineering comparison: conventional DFB has a fibre-coupled packaged boundary, while SOA-DFB has higher reported PCE but a less complete package boundary. The numbers cannot be ranked without matched coupling, lifetime and qualification data.
4. PAP-021's 1.51 pJ/bit is a compact device-boundary result at 56 Gb/s and excludes the laser. It cannot be compared directly with the project's 102.4T chassis scenarios or with module-level LPO power.
5. PAP-024's most investable implication is process control: coupler loss, fine-pitch bonding, thermal pathways and wafer-level test determine whether a high-performance PIC becomes a low-cost, qualified engine.

## Required next measurements

For each candidate 200G/lane engine, request the same fields: lane rate and modulation, BER/FEC and TDECQ, reach and host loss, complete optical and electrical power including laser/control, optical output and coupling loss distribution, temperature range, package/connector boundary, sample size, first-pass and final good-engine yield, qualification status, cycle time, ASP, warranty and field-replacement method.
