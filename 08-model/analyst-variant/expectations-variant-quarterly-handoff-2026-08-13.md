# Expectations-versus-variant quarterly handoff — 13 August 2026

**Status:** Private operating control; no price target, public consensus detail, or CPO EPS output  
**Scope:** NVIDIA, Broadcom, Coherent, Lumentum, Marvell and TSMC

## Purpose

This handoff connects three records that must remain separate: reported company results and the public consolidated denominator; external expectations, including management targets and lawfully retained analyst material; and Nur Alpys’ variant, which can be qualitative even when no numeric CPO overlay is eligible.

It prevents a company result from being treated as consensus, a management target from being treated as an observed outcome, or a restricted estimate from being treated as a public CPO fact.

## Required quarterly sequence

| Step | Required record | Pass condition | Prohibited shortcut |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Fix the clock | Review/as-of date plus company fiscal-period map | Every result and expectation carries a date and fiscal label | Comparing calendar-year growth across different fiscal periods |
| 2. Record observed outcome | Filing/results anchor with GAAP/non-GAAP basis and unit | Reported result is tied to `FIL-###`/claim ID | Calling company guidance or consensus a reported result |
| 3. Refresh expectations | Complete `EST-###`/`ANL-###` rows, or retain management expectation separately | Source, restriction, as-of date, fiscal period, metric, unit and public-use status are present | Averaging management targets, stale estimates and incompatible accounting bases |
| 4. Measure the change | Prior expectation/variant versus new reported result and current expectation | Change is labelled achieved, missed, delayed, withdrawn or unchanged | Treating silence, availability, or a product demo as delivery/earnings conversion |
| 5. Recheck CPO gates | Exact product/customer, units, repeatability, content, yield, service and economics | Gate register is updated only by product-matched evidence | Using an estimate to fill a missing customer numerator, share or margin |
| 6. Write the variant | Fact, expectation, assumption and inference are labelled separately | One catalyst and one falsification condition are explicit | Publishing a numeric CPO EPS/valuation output while any critical input is blocked |
| 7. Release review | Source licence and public/private boundary are checked | Only derived, permitted conclusions may leave the private workspace | Copying analyst report tables, pages, names or exact protected figures |

## Company update card: minimum quarterly fields

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Reported baseline | Period, source ID, revenue/margin/cash/share fields actually reported, accounting basis |
| External expectation | `EXP-###` or `EST-###`, source/as-of date, unit, fiscal period and access/public-use status |
| Commercial CPO state | Exact product/domain; customer, units, repeat, supplier and economics gates as `open`, `partial` or `cleared` |
| Variant view | Positive/neutral/negative *relative CPO stance* plus confidence; not a stock recommendation |
| Attribution | CPO variable affected, or explicit `not eligible` |
| Change assessment | Achieved, missed, delayed, withdrawn or unchanged—with source IDs |
| Catalyst / falsification | Next observable record and the evidence that invalidates the view |

## Present application

The current six-company cards meet the qualitative version of this handoff: reported anchors exist, management/external expectation records are separated, and each stance carries a catalyst and falsification condition. The numeric version is **not eligible** because no company has a reconciled CPO numerator, product economics and complete fiscal/share/tax/valuation baseline.

`ANL-002` remains a provisional consolidated-scale snapshot, not a CPO consensus set. It must be refreshed by 8 November 2026 or moved to historical context. A new licensed report can be added only through the analyst-estimate register and must remain private unless its licence explicitly permits a published derived range.

## Change taxonomy

| Label | Use when | Example CPO treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Achieved | A dated target is met with a product-matched observed record | Still verify whether the result names product, customer, units and economics |
| Missed | A dated target passes without evidence of the stated outcome | Downgrade timing confidence; do not infer failure if disclosure is incomplete |
| Delayed | Company/customer explicitly moves timing | Preserve prior target and record the new date and reason |
| Withdrawn | Company withdraws or materially changes the target/product route | Re-open affected commercial, manufacturing and economics gates |
| Unchanged | No matched evidence moves the gate | Preserve the prior state; no narrative progress from repetition |
