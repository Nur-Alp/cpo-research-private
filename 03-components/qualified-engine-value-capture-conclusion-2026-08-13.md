# Qualified-engine value-capture conclusion

**Status:** Private investability conclusion; no company profit ranking  
**As of:** 2026-08-13

## Conclusion

**Inference, medium confidence:** test/burn-in is the clearest observable manufacturing readiness/control point; fibre interface and package geometry are the leading unresolved yield risks. No layer has public evidence of durable CPO profit capture. The constrained step can create value only if it is scarce, qualified, product-linked and priced above its yield/warranty/capital burden.

| Layer | Evidence of technical/control relevance | Evidence of durable economics | Current investment read |
|---|---|---|---|
| PIC design/IP | Strong technical relevance; integration route matters | No product-linked supplier share/ASP/margin | Potential strategic control, not proven profit pool |
| External lasers | Formal ELSFP service boundary and component qualification signals | Customer/product allocation, warranty and margin absent | Most legible component route; not whole-engine economics |
| Connector/FAU | Dense-interface and service boundary matter | Yield, mating, rework and supplier economics absent | High diligence priority; no value-capture call |
| OSAT/package | Warpage, thermal and integration controls matter | Final yield, cost, rework and OSAT margin absent | Potential bottleneck; economics unproven |
| Test/burn-in | Multiple production-oriented tools and capacity signals | No CPO-specific installed-output/cost/profit allocation | Clearest observable readiness control point, not a profit leader |
| Platform integration | NVIDIA/Broadcom control product routes | CPO-specific platform or supplier margin absent | Strongest strategic positions; economic share open |

## What would promote a layer

1. Named product/customer boundary.
2. Qualified output or service population.
3. Product-matched yield/rework/warranty record.
4. Attributable price, supplier share and gross-margin/capex boundary.

Until all four converge, treat the layer as a **watch point**, not a CPO earnings driver.

## Disconfirming evidence

This conclusion changes if a product-matched waterfall shows attach/package/test loss is immaterial, or if an identified supplier discloses accepted output, price and margin at the constrained stage.
