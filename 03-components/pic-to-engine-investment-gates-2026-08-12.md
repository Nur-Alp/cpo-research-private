# PIC-to-engine investment gates — 12 August 2026

**Status:** Private decision control; not a technology ranking
**Scope:** Silicon photonics, InP, thin-film lithium niobate and heterogeneous integration

See the [PIC investment-boundary audit](pic-investment-boundary-audit-2026-08-12.md)
for the rung-by-rung test that prevents device or prototype evidence from
being promoted into a supplier ranking.

## Core distinction

A PIC can demonstrate bandwidth, loss or temperature performance without
proving that a supplier can deliver a qualified engine at attractive economics.
The investable unit is a **customer-accepted, serviceable good engine** with a
defined laser, EIC, coupling, package and test boundary.

## Route-to-engine handoff

| Route | Device evidence | Engine boundary still needed | Economic gate | Current permitted conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Silicon photonics + external laser | Strongest switch-CPO process/product relevance; external-light, screening and attach routes disclosed | Complete PIC/EIC/laser/attach/package/test map for the exact SKU; final-engine yield and service workflow | Transfer price, qualified share, laser lifetime, rework, warranty and product margin | Leading 200G switch-CPO diligence route; no proven supplier winner |
| InP | Monolithic 200G-class and TEC-less 400G-class transmission records | Complete multi-channel engine power, coupling, package, test and replacement boundary | Compound-semiconductor yield, ASP, qualification, rework and margin | Serious modular countercase; no universal CPO conclusion |
| Thin-film lithium niobate | 225GBd/400G-class transmission evidence | Product-level laser, receiver, package, fibre attach and service boundary | Wafer-to-module yield, qualification, test cost, ASP and margin | High-rate modular countercase; production economics open |
| Heterogeneous integration | Interposer, FOWLP, TGV, chiplet and known-good-die process records | Matched final package/engine architecture and ownership of each layer | Final-engine Cpk/yield, rework, capex, supplier share and price | Potential manufacturing control point; value capture unproven |

## Five gates before an investment conclusion

1. **Performance gate:** measured multi-channel operation at the target lane
   rate, reach, BER/FEC and temperature—not a best-channel or component result.
2. **Integration gate:** laser, EIC, PIC, coupling, package and test are mapped
   to one product boundary without double counting.
3. **Production gate:** starts, screened units, assembled engines, final-test
   pass, rework and accepted/shipped units have denominators.
4. **Service gate:** replaceable failure domains, MTTR, spares, warranty and
   field exposure are known.
5. **Economic gate:** realised content/ASP, qualified supplier share, yield
   burden, product margin, R&D and attributable capex are known or explicitly
   scenario-only.

## What current evidence supports

- Silicon photonics with external light is the most relevant current
  switch-CPO process route at 200G/lane.
- InP and TFLN provide credible higher-rate modular countercases, especially
  where serviceability and electrical reach remain decisive.
- Heterogeneous integration may control a critical packaging/test step, but
  process control is not proof of complete-engine revenue or profit.

These are evidence-adjusted research priorities, not claims that any route or
company will capture the profit pool.

## Falsification conditions

Downgrade the SiPh/CPO process-control view if a qualified InP/TFLN/pluggable
system matches the same 200G/400G boundary at lower cost per accepted or
restored port, or if CPO final-engine yield/service economics remain inferior.
Downgrade any PIC supplier thesis if its role is only a broad partnership or
capacity reservation without exact-SKU qualification and realised share.
