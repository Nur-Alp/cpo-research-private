## What the document establishes

CoreWeave describes a network backbone built around high-speed connectivity and cites Quantum-X800, Quantum-2 and Spectrum-X networking in its infrastructure. It describes closed-loop liquid cooling and planned deployment of Rubin-generation systems. These disclosures establish the operator's platform and infrastructure context, but do not isolate the optical implementation of any named switch.

## Evidence boundary

The filing does not disclose CPO switch units, exact optical SKU, engine/PIC supplier, qualification lot, yield, repeat shipment, field-return distribution, ASP, margin or CPO revenue. References to Spectrum-X or Quantum-X in a company-wide risk/business description must not be converted into a CPO deployment numerator.

## Investment-model use

Use as a primary operator-scale and infrastructure-context record, alongside `FIL-012`. Do not use reported CoreWeave revenue, backlog, margins or capex as a proxy for CPO economics.
