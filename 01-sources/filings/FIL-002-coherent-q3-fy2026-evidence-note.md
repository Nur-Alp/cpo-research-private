# FIL-002 — Coherent Q3 FY2026 financial evidence

- **Period:** quarter ended 28 March 2026; release furnished 6 May 2026.
- **Primary record:** [local SEC HTML](FIL-002-coherent-q3-fy2026-8k.html) | [canonical SEC record](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/820318/000119312526208972/d57080dex991.htm)

## What the filing establishes

Coherent reported Q3 FY2026 revenue of **$1.806 billion**, GAAP gross margin of **37.7%**, and non-GAAP gross margin of **39.6%**. It guided Q4 revenue to **$1.91–2.05 billion** and non-GAAP gross margin to **39–41%**. The release describes strong datacenter/communications demand and capacity expansion.

## CPO boundary

The filing is a consolidated company result. It does **not** disclose CPO revenue, optical-engine units, CPO ASP, CPO gross margin, customer qualification, attach yield, or CPO-attributed capex. The figures are therefore denominators for materiality analysis, not a CPO profitability proxy.
