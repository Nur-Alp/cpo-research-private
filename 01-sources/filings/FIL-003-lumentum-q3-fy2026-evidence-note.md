# FIL-003 — Lumentum Q3 FY2026 financial evidence

- **Period:** quarter and nine months ended 28 March 2026; Form 10-Q filed 6 May 2026.
- **Primary record:** [local SEC HTML](FIL-003-lumentum-q3-fy2026-10q.html) | [canonical SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633978/000162828026030777/lite-20260328.htm)

## What the filing establishes

Lumentum reported Q3 revenue of **$808.4 million** and Q3 GAAP gross margin of **44.2%** (nine-month gross margin **38.8%**). The quarter's gross-margin improvement was attributed 46% to manufacturing-cost/utilization gains, 37% to mix, and 17% to lower acquired-intangible amortization; the nine-month attribution was 57%/23%/20%. Nine-month capex was **$284.5 million**, versus $177.1 million in the prior-year period.

The filing also reports Q3 increases of **$232.5 million in Components** and **$150.7 million in Systems**, cloud-transceiver revenue growth of about **$137 million**, and optical-circuit-switch shipments above **$25 million** in the quarter and **$38 million** year-to-date. Two customers represented 26% and 12% of quarterly revenue (24% and 16% for nine months).

## CPO boundary

These are broad company, product-category, and customer-concentration disclosures. They do **not** identify CPO revenue, optical-engine units, CPO ASP, CPO gross margin, qualified capacity, attach yield, warranty cost, or CPO-attributed capex. Customer identities and CPO allocation are undisclosed.

## Research use

Use the record for utilization, mix, capital-intensity, component/system, and concentration context—not as evidence that Lumentum has a reported CPO profit pool.
