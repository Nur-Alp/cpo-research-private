# Celestica Q1 2026 Form 10-Q — evidence note

Source: [Celestica Q1 2026 Form 10-Q (SEC HTML)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1030894/000103089426000032/cls-20260331.htm)

The original SEC HTML is retained locally as `FIL-009-celestica-q1-2026-10q.html`.

## Decision-relevant observations

- Celestica describes its Connectivity & Cloud Solutions segment as serving cloud providers, hyperscalers, data-centre customers and OEMs, with end-to-end design, manufacturing, supply-chain, testing, integration and fulfilment capabilities.
- Q1 2026 Hardware Platform Solutions revenue was approximately $1.7 billion, or 42% of total revenue, driven by networking switch programmes with hyperscaler customers. The filing says 2026 capital spending is expected to be approximately $1 billion, with most Q1 2026 capex supporting CCS and AI/ML/HPS programmes.
- Celestica reports Q1 2026 gross margin of 10.8% and CCS segment margin of 8.6%. These are company/segment denominators, not CPO economics.
- Customer concentration is high: the top ten customers represented 84% of Q1 2026 revenue, and three CCS customers individually represented 35%, 15% and 15%.
- The 10-Q does not name CPO or co-packaged optics. The CPO Ethernet-switch programme appears in the separate Q1 results release (`CMP-028`), so this filing is useful for the financial and execution boundary but cannot attribute CPO revenue or margin.

## Boundary

Do not apply Celestica's HPS revenue, CCS margin, capex or customer concentration directly to the unnamed CPO programme. The proper use is as a denominator and risk screen until product-level allocation is disclosed.
