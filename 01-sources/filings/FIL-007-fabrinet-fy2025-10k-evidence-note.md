# Fabrinet FY2025 Form 10-K — evidence note

Source: [Fabrinet FY2025 Form 10-K (SEC HTML)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1408710/000140871025000039/fn-20250627.htm)

The original SEC HTML is retained locally as `FIL-007-fabrinet-fy2025-10k.html`. The filing is useful for the manufacturing-route and profit-pool workstream, but it does not identify a CPO-specific programme.

## Decision-relevant observations

- Fabrinet describes a full outsourced manufacturing flow spanning process design, supply chain, advanced optical packaging, integration, final assembly and testing. It says it is often the sole outsourced manufacturing partner for a customer’s products.
- Its Silicon Valley and Israel NPI centres are intended to support design-for-manufacturability and transfer qualified programmes to Thailand for volume manufacturing. The filing says some products require three to six months or longer of environmental and reliability qualification before volume production.
- FY2025 revenue was $3.419 billion; optical communications represented $2.619 billion, or 76.6% of revenue. Gross profit was $413.3 million (12.1% margin), operating income $324.4 million (9.5%), and capital expenditures $130.7 million, including a new Chonburi building and equipment expansion.
- Two customers each represented at least 10% of revenue and together represented 45.8% of FY2025 revenue. Fabrinet says customer commitments generally do not extend beyond 13 weeks and business under master supply agreements is awarded project by project.

## Boundary

These facts establish a plausible OSAT/contract-manufacturing control point and its customer leverage, capacity and qualification economics. They do not establish that Fabrinet manufactures a named CPO engine, owns the PIC or laser, has a specific NVIDIA/Broadcom allocation, or earns a particular CPO margin.
