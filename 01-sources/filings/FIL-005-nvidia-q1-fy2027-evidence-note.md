# FIL-005 — NVIDIA Q1 FY2027 financial evidence

- **Period:** quarter ended 26 April 2026; Form 10-Q filed 28 May 2026.
- **Primary record:** [local SEC HTML](FIL-005-nvidia-q1-fy2027-10q.html) | [canonical SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000052/nvda-20260426.htm) | [official results release](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-First-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/default.aspx)

## What the filing establishes

NVIDIA reported Q1 FY2027 revenue of **$81.615 billion** and Data Center revenue of **$75.178 billion**. The release breaks Data Center into approximately **$60.4 billion compute** and **$14.8 billion networking**, with GAAP gross margin of **74.9%** and Q2 revenue guidance of **$91.0 billion ±2%**. The 10-Q attributes Data Center growth to Blackwell, InfiniBand, Spectrum-X Ethernet, and NVLink solutions.

## CPO boundary

Data Center and networking are broad categories. Neither the filing nor the release discloses CPO revenue, optical-engine content, CPO gross margin, system count, supplier share, capex, or earnings contribution. Platform/networking leadership is therefore not proof of CPO profit-pool capture.

## Research use

Use this record to set NVIDIA's earnings-materiality denominator and to avoid attributing all networking growth to CPO.
