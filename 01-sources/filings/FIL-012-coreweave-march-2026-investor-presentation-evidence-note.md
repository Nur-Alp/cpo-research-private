## What the document establishes

The presentation describes CoreWeave's financing and deployment scale. It reports $66.8 billion of revenue backlog at December 31, 2025, approximately 40% expected within 24 months and approximately 80% within 48 months. It describes a five-year weighted-average contract life, 43 active data centers, more than 850 MW of active power and more than 3.1 GW of contracted power as of December 31, 2025. It also describes a repeat-and-expand motion under existing master service agreements and an illustrative deployment ramp in which revenue begins after provisioning and testing.

## Evidence boundary

This is **operator-scale context**, not CPO shipment evidence. The presentation does not identify CPO switch SKUs, optical-engine units, CPO qualification, supplier allocation, repeat CPO shipments, CPO ASP, CPO margin or CPO-specific service history. The backlog and power figures therefore cannot be used as a CPO numerator. They can be used to define the scale of a named customer/operator and to test whether future CPO claims are commensurate with the operator's stated buildout.

## Investment-model use

Use this record for the customer-scale denominator and deployment-economics context. Keep it separate from CoreWeave's CPO adoption records (`CMP-021`, `CMP-039`, `CMP-046`).
