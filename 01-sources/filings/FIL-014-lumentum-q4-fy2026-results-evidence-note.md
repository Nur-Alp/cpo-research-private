## Relevant CPO / external-light statement

Lumentum's CEO said that “increasing demand for ultra-high-power CPO lasers” and “an initial order for ELS modules” are early signs of optics penetrating in-rack connectivity. This is a company earnings-release statement. It identifies an initial ELS-module order but does **not** state the customer, product/SKU, CPO platform, order value, quantity, delivery date, revenue-recognition timing, supplier share, price, gross margin, cancellation terms, yield or warranty allocation.

## Financial context

For Q4 FY2026, the release reported net revenue of $1.0063bn, GAAP gross margin of 47.4%, and non-GAAP gross margin of 50.4%. It reported FY2026 net revenue of $3.0140bn, GAAP gross margin of 41.7%, and non-GAAP gross margin of 46.0%.

Those are consolidated-company results. The release reports component and systems revenue categories but does not isolate CPO, ELS modules, ultra-high-power CPO lasers, customer allocation, CPO revenue or CPO product margin. The reported gross margins therefore cannot be entered as CPO/ELS gross-margin inputs.

## Decision treatment

This advances the Lumentum external-light commercial-route record from an order disclosed for future H1 2027 CPO delivery to a later company statement that an initial ELS-module order exists. It does not clear a CPO product, customer, unit, repeatability, supplier-content or profit-pool gate.
