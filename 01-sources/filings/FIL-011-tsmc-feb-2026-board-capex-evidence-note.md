# TSMC February 2026 board resolution — capacity evidence note

Source: [TSMC Board of Directors Meeting Resolutions](https://pr.tsmc.com/english/news/3287)

The official page was reviewed through the publisher URL; direct local retention was unavailable. The canonical link and this evidence note preserve the source boundary.

## Decision-relevant observations

- TSMC's board approved approximately US$44.962 billion of capital appropriations for long-term capacity plans, including advanced-technology capacity, advanced packaging, mature/specialty capacity and fab construction/facility systems.
- This is a material capacity commitment relevant to the manufacturing-control-point thesis, but it is not a COUPE-specific investment or a CPO revenue forecast.
- The resolution does not identify the share allocated to COUPE, CoWoS, optical engines, photonic packaging, customers or product lines.

## Boundary

Treat the amount as a company-level capacity ceiling/context input only. Do not allocate it to CPO or use it to infer TSMC CPO profit, yield or output.
