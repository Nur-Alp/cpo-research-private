# FIL-004 — Broadcom Q2 FY2026 financial evidence

- **Period:** quarter ended 3 May 2026; Form 10-Q filed 3 June 2026.
- **Primary record:** [local SEC HTML](FIL-004-broadcom-q2-fy2026-10q.html) | [canonical SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1730168/000173016826000054/avgo-20260503.htm) | [official results release](https://investors.broadcom.com/news-releases/news-release-details/broadcom-inc-announces-second-quarter-fiscal-year-2026-financial)

## What the filing establishes

Broadcom reported Q2 FY2026 revenue of **$22.187 billion**. Company-reported AI semiconductor revenue was **$10.8 billion**, up 143% year over year; free cash flow was **$10.262 billion**, adjusted EBITDA **$15.244 billion**, and capex **$231 million**. The company guided Q3 revenue to **$29.4 billion** and Q3 AI-semiconductor revenue to **$16 billion**.

## CPO boundary

AI semiconductor is a company-defined category spanning more than CPO. The filing and results release do **not** disclose CPO revenue, optical-engine content, CPO gross margin, switch-CPO units, supplier share, CPO capex, or CPO earnings contribution.

## Research use

Use Broadcom's figures as a denominator and AI-investment context. They cannot establish that switch CPO is material to Broadcom earnings or that Broadcom captures the optical-engine profit pool.
