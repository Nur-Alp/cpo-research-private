# TSMC Q2 2026 results — evidence note

Source: [TSMC Q2 2026 results](https://pr.tsmc.com/japanese/news/3326)

The official English-language results page was not directly retrievable in this environment; the canonical TSMC page is retained in the source log and this note preserves the reviewed boundary.

## Decision-relevant observations

- TSMC reported Q2 2026 revenue of US$40.20 billion, gross margin of 67.7%, operating margin of 60.3% and net profit margin of 55.6%.
- Advanced technologies (7 nm and more advanced) represented 77% of wafer revenue. Management guided Q3 2026 revenue to US$44.6–45.8 billion and gross margin to 65–67%.
- These figures establish a large, high-margin company denominator for testing whether COUPE/CPO revenue could matter to TSMC. They do not isolate COUPE, advanced packaging, optical engines or CPO economics.

## Boundary

Do not apply TSMC's consolidated margins to a CPO engine. The source supplies earnings scale only; customer SKU, COUPE output, package responsibility, ASP, yield, margin and capex allocation remain open.
