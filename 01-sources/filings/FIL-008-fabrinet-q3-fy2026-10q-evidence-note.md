# Fabrinet Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q — evidence note

Source: [Fabrinet Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q (SEC HTML)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1408710/000140871026000016/fn-20260327.htm)

The original SEC HTML is retained locally as `FIL-008-fabrinet-q3-fy2026-10q.html`.

## Decision-relevant observations

- For the quarter ended March 27, 2026, Fabrinet reported $196.9 million of datacenter-interconnect revenue and $106.7 million of high-performance-computing revenue. For the first nine months, the corresponding figures were $477.2 million and $207.7 million.
- The filing defines DCI modules as opto-electronic modules connecting two or more geographically separated data centres. It attributes quarterly optical-communications growth partly to DCI demand and non-optical growth partly to HPC demand.
- Fabrinet says customer contracts typically provide fixed quarterly pricing that assumes specific production yields and quality metrics. It warns that lower yields, defects, correction costs, warranty claims and customer penalties can reduce margins.
- As of March 27, 2026, total capital-expenditure commitments were $164.7 million, including a $132.5 million Chonburi manufacturing-building contract. These commitments are not identified as CPO-related.

## Boundary

The filing strengthens the case that Fabrinet has material exposure to datacenter interconnect and a useful view of yield/warranty economics. It still does not identify a CPO engine, PIC, laser, customer SKU, manufacturing allocation or CPO-specific margin.
