## Financial result retained

Coherent reported fiscal-Q4 2026 revenue of **$2.05bn**, GAAP gross margin of
**38.5%**, non-GAAP gross margin of **40.2%**, and GAAP EPS of $1.19. The
release says the fiscal quarter ended 30 June 2026.

## CPO-economic boundary

The release says Coherent expects to benefit from broad AI-datacenter optical
connectivity and is expanding production capacity. It does **not** name a CPO
product, customer, engine/PIC/laser allocation, product output, price, yield,
rework, warranty, CPO revenue, product margin, or CPO-specific capex.

The result is therefore a current consolidated materiality denominator only.
It must not be applied to an optical engine, a laser, a PIC, a package, or any
CPO profit-pool sensitivity.

## Decision treatment

Update the company-scale context for Coherent. Do not change the component
content, customer, output, or CPO-economic gates.
