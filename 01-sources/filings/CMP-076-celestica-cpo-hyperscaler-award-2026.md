# CMP-076 — Celestica CPO switch program hyperscaler award

**Canonical source:** https://corporate.celestica.com/node/17386/pdf
**Reviewed:** 2026-08-12

Celestica's Q1 2026 results disclose an award for a co-packaged-optics switch
program with a hyperscaler customer and state that the HPS program is expected
to begin ramping production in 2027.

The customer is unnamed and the disclosure does not identify the exact ASIC,
CPO SKU, Broadcom TH6 mapping, accepted units, repeat shipments, supplier
content, yield, ASP, margin, field service or warranty. Treat it as a concrete
integrator production-ramp lead, not customer-accepted volume evidence.
