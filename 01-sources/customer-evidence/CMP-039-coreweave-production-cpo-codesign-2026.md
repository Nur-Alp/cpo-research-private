# CMP-039 — CoreWeave/NVIDIA production co-design and Photonics CPO adoption

- **Publisher:** CoreWeave
- **Canonical source:** https://www.coreweave.com/blog/why-ai-factories-need-proof-before-production
- **Publication:** 2026-07-29
- **Evidence class:** Customer/operator technical post

CoreWeave describes a co-designed, pre-deployment validation process with NVIDIA and says it is an early adopter of NVIDIA Photonics co-packaged-optics networking. The post separately states that CoreWeave was among the first cloud providers to deploy the NVIDIA Spectrum-X Ethernet SN6600-LD 102.4 Tb/s switch, while the CPO statement is not tied to a disclosed unit count or SKU.

**Evidence boundary:** This strengthens customer/operator adoption and validation evidence, but it does not identify CPO units, exact engine/PIC/laser content, qualification completion, repeat shipments, field failure rates, ASP or margin. Spectrum-X switch deployment and Photonics CPO adoption must remain separate claims.
