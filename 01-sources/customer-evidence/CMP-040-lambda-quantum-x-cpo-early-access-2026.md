# CMP-040 — Lambda Quantum-X Photonics CPO early-access installation record

- **Publisher:** Lambda
- **Canonical source:** https://lambda.ai/blog/unbox-one-of-nvidias-first-co-packaged-optics-switches-with-lambda
- **Publication:** 2026-06-01
- **Evidence class:** Customer/operator early-access technical post

Lambda reports an early look at an NVIDIA Quantum-X InfiniBand Photonics Q3450-LD engineering sample. The post describes a 4U, 115.2 Tb/s non-blocking switch with 144 × 800G InfiniBand ports, 144 MPO connectors and 18 removable external light-source modules, each feeding eight MPO ports. Lambda explains that lasers remain external for field serviceability while the rest of the optical path is inside the sealed package, and documents rack integration, liquid cooling, busbar power and fibre-management work.

**Evidence boundary:** This is unusually concrete operational evidence for an early-access CPO sample and service boundary, not repeat production. The disclosed power comparison (3.95 kW CPO versus 7.0 kW standard switch) is a customer-reported comparison with an undisclosed measurement boundary; it must not be substituted for the repository’s matched 102.4T model. No unit count, qualification completion, production schedule, engine yield, field returns, ASP or margin is disclosed.
