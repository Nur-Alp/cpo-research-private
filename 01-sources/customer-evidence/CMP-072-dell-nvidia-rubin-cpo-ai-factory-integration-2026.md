# CMP-072 — Dell/NVIDIA Rubin AI Factory CPO integration

- **Publisher:** Dell Technologies
- **Canonical source:** https://www.dell.com/en-us/blog/deploy-ai-faster-with-integrated-compute-and-networking-from-dell-and-nvidia/
- **Publication:** 2026-03-16 (updated 2026-04-29)
- **Evidence class:** OEM/platform integration statement; not a customer shipment record

Dell describes its AI Factory with NVIDIA as an integrated rack-scale architecture
combining Rubin compute, PowerSwitch SN6000 networking and liquid cooling. The
article identifies co-packaged-optics options in the SN6000 series, illustrates
the PowerSwitch SN6800-LD, and says these systems are paired with Dell's
validated designs and deployment services.

**Evidence boundary:** The article establishes an OEM integration and product
route. It does not identify a customer accepting `SN6800-LD` or `SN6810-LD`,
provide an order/acceptance date, units/ports, repeat shipment, field-service
population, supplier allocation, yield, ASP or margin. A team.blue quotation
on the same page concerns Dell SONiC across NVIDIA Spectrum platforms and is
not evidence of a CPO deployment.

**Decision treatment:** Use as independent OEM integration evidence and as a
false-positive control against associating a customer quotation with the
illustrated CPO SKU. Do not count it as exact-SKU customer proof.
