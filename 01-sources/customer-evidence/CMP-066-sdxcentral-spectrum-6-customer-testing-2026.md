# CMP-066 — SDxCentral Spectrum-6 customer-testing report

- **Publisher:** SDxCentral
- **Canonical source:** https://www.sdxcentral.com/news/nvidia-debuts-spectrum-6-switches-to-power-next-gen-ai-networking/
- **Publication:** 2026-07-21
- **Evidence class:** Secondary trade-press report; retained as a lead, not a primary commercial record

SDxCentral reports that Microsoft, CoreWeave, Nebius and SpaceXAI were listed
among initial AI-infrastructure operators set to deploy NVIDIA Spectrum-6
switch lines. It separately says that several customers had already been
testing the CPO switches. The article identifies the SN6810 and SN6800 product
families and describes Spectrum-6 as supporting both pluggable and CPO forms.

**Evidence boundary:** This is useful triangulation that named operators were
associated with the Spectrum-6 launch and that customer testing was reported.
The article does not identify which named operator tested or accepted the CPO
configuration, does not name `SN6810-LD` or `SN6800-LD` in a customer
deployment, and supplies no accepted units/ports, date of acceptance, repeat
shipment, service record or economics. The Lambda example in the same article
is explicitly the different Quantum-X InfiniBand `Q3450-LD` product.

**Decision treatment:** Do not count this as exact-SKU customer proof. Use it to
target primary follow-up with the named operators and OEMs, while preserving
the current open commercial numerator.
