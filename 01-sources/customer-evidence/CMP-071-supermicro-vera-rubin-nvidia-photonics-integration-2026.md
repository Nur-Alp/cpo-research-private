# CMP-071 — Supermicro Vera Rubin / NVIDIA Photonics CPO integration

- **Publisher:** Supermicro
- **Canonical source:** https://learn-more.supermicro.com/data-center-stories/ai-infrastructure-cpo-nvidia-photonics-switches-vera-rubin-supermicro-dcbbs?hs_amp=true
- **Publication:** 2026-06-01
- **Evidence class:** OEM integration statement; not a shipment or acceptance record

Supermicro says it is integrating NVIDIA Spectrum-X Ethernet Photonics and
Quantum-X InfiniBand Photonics switches into Vera Rubin NVL72 and Rubin NVL8
platform solutions through its Data Center Building Block Solutions framework.
It describes the offering as a pre-validated, rack-scale solution combining
compute, networking and liquid cooling for production environments.

**Evidence boundary:** This is a useful OEM integration and deployment-readiness
signal. The page does not identify an exact `SN6800`/`SN6810` or `-LD` customer
order, accepted units/ports, shipment date, repeat deployment, field-service
population, supplier allocation, yield, ASP or margin. It also groups
Spectrum-X and Quantum-X and therefore does not isolate the Ethernet CPO
configuration.

**Decision treatment:** Add to the NVIDIA follow-up queue as an OEM-integrated
deployment lead. Do not count it as exact-SKU customer proof or as a CPO
commercial numerator.
