## What the source establishes

Lumentum plans to retrofit a 240,000-square-foot Greensboro, North Carolina site to manufacture InP optical products including CW and UHP lasers. NVIDIA is identified as a customer of the facility, and Lumentum says the fab will also support other AI-infrastructure customers. The facility is planned to use 6-inch InP wafers, ramp production in mid-2028, and require hundreds of millions of dollars of investment over several years.

## Evidence boundary

This is planned capacity, not qualified output. The announcement gives no wafer starts, die yield, product mix, customer allocation, cost per good device, qualification, ASP or margin. “NVIDIA will serve as a customer” does not specify which products or volumes.

## Research use

Add a dated capacity milestone and capex input to the Lumentum case. Model mid-2028 as a planned ramp checkpoint, not as shipped CPO volume.
