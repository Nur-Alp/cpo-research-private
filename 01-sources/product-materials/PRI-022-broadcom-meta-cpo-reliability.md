# Broadcom/Meta CPO reliability milestone — primary-source review

- **Publisher:** Broadcom Inc.
- **Publication date:** 1 October 2025
- **Canonical source:** <https://www.broadcom.com/company/news/product-releases/63616>
- **Original:** [Official one-page PDF](https://investors.broadcom.com/node/63616/pdf) (canonical link retained; the publisher endpoint returned HTTP 403 to local retrieval on 7 August 2026)
- **Reviewed:** 7 August 2026

## What the source establishes

Broadcom reports one million cumulative 400G-equivalent port-device hours of flap-free CPO operation at Meta. The release describes the observation as occurring in Meta's high-temperature lab characterization environment and reports a 65% optics-power reduction versus pluggable solutions. Broadcom characterizes this as production-readiness and says its qualification process covered electrical, optical and mechanical domains.

## Evidence boundary

This is a company release citing a company presentation and a customer test environment. It is valuable primary evidence, but it is not an independently audited field-reliability dataset. The release does not provide the number of ports or devices, wall-clock duration, censoring rules, failure definition beyond link flaps, temperature profile, confidence interval, fleet-production status, repair history, warranty allocation, yield, ASP or margin.

## Research use

Upgrade the reliability signal from secondary reporting to a dated company/customer-reported primary observation. Keep the field-reliability, service-cost and commercial-proof gates open until population, qualification, production deployment and repair economics are disclosed.
