# CMP-067 — Lumentum NVIDIA silicon-photonics ecosystem role

- **Publisher:** Lumentum
- **Canonical source:** https://investor.lumentum.com/financial-news-releases/news-details/2025/Lumentum-Selected-as-an-NVIDIA-Silicon-Photonics-Ecosystem-Partner-to-Advance-AI-Networking-at-Scale/default.aspx
- **Publication:** 2025-03-18
- **Evidence class:** Supplier company announcement

Lumentum says it was selected as a key contributor in NVIDIA's silicon-photonics
ecosystem and that its high-power, high-efficiency lasers have a crucial role
in the development and deployment of NVIDIA Spectrum-X Photonics and
Quantum-X Photonics networking switches.

**Evidence boundary:** This is a supplier-to-platform role statement that
supports Lumentum as a confirmed NVIDIA laser ecosystem participant. It does
not identify the exact production SKU, laser die/module allocation, qualified
share, order value, unit volume, yield, warranty, ASP or product margin. Do not
convert “crucial role” into sole-source status or CPO profit leadership.
