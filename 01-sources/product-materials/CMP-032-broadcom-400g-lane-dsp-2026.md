# CMP-021 — Broadcom Taurus 400G/lane optical DSP

Canonical publisher page: <https://investors.broadcom.com/news-releases/news-release-details/broadcom-delivers-industrys-first-400glane-optical-dsp-next>

Reviewed 2026-08-07. Broadcom announced availability of the 3 nm Taurus BCM83640 400G/lane PAM-4 DSP for 1.6T transceivers. The release states an integrated laser driver, interoperability with Broadcom 400G EML and photodiode devices, support for 1.6T–3.2T modules, and sampling to early-access customers and partners. It describes 400G/lane as enabling 102.4T switching and a future 204.8T/3.2T path.

Evidence boundary: availability and sampling are product-stage claims, not measured end-to-end 400G/lane LPO/NPO/CPO proof. The release does not disclose customer qualification, channel-loss budget, production yield, field reliability, ASP, or gross margin.
