# CMP-068 — SENKO NVIDIA photonics connector ecosystem role

- **Publisher:** SENKO Advanced Components
- **Canonical source:** https://www.senko.com/senko-advanced-components-joins-nvidia-photonics-ecosystem/
- **Publication:** 2025-03-18
- **Evidence class:** Supplier company announcement

SENKO says it participates in NVIDIA's photonics ecosystem and supplies
high-speed detachable fibre-optic connectors for NVIDIA Spectrum-X Photonics
and Quantum-X Photonics networking switches. It describes silicon-photonics
PIC connectors as low-loss detachable connections to the chip.

**Evidence boundary:** This supports a confirmed connector ecosystem role for
the two NVIDIA photonics families, but not a specific `SN6800`/`SN6810` order,
qualified share, connector loss distribution, mating-life qualification,
volume, ASP, warranty or margin. It is not evidence that SENKO supplies the
complete fibre-attach process or optical engine.
