## Evidence retained

The official product brief defines the switch boundary:

- 102.4Tb/s maximum capacity: up to 64 × 1.6TbE, 128 × 800GbE, 256 × 400GbE or 512 × 200GbE ports;
- a maximum of 64 integrated 3nm Condor SerDes cores, each with eight 212.5Gb/s PAM4 transceivers and associated PCS;
- 512 × 200G PAM4 all-optical I/O;
- 512 duplex single-mode fibres to the front panel, each stated as single-wavelength 1310nm and directly driven from the Condor SerDes through the integrated optical engines.

## Evidence boundary

This is a vendor product brief. It confirms the **ASIC/SerDes/fibre/interface boundary**, but not the supplier of the PIC, EIC, laser, package, fibre attach, connector or test flow. It does not establish a qualified customer, shipped/accepted systems or ports, engine yield, rework, warranty, ASP, margin, production mix or repeat deployment. Its power and cost language is a Broadcom product claim, not a matched system comparison.

## Research use

Use for `CLM-516`–`CLM-517`, the BCM78919 product boundary and the supplier-content map. Do not use its 512-fibre/64-core architecture to assign third-party engine revenue or to treat the device as volume deployed.
