# CMP-083 — NVIDIA silicon-photonics CPO role map

## Retained source

- **Publisher:** NVIDIA Technical Blog
- **Title:** *A New Era in Data Center Networking with NVIDIA Silicon Photonics-based Network Switching*
- **Author:** Brad Smith
- **Publication date:** 2025-03-27
- **Canonical URL:** <https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/a-new-era-in-data-center-networking-with-nvidia-silicon-photonics-based-network-switching/>
- **Access checked:** 2026-08-13

## Evidence retained

NVIDIA describes a CPO manufacturing and integration ecosystem for its
silicon-photonics-based network switching. It states that TSMC's COUPE process
integrates EICs and PICs; that SPIL performs wafer bumping, wafer sort,
assembly and testing for the NVIDIA CPO multi-chip module; and that Lumentum,
Sumitomo and Coherent provide external-laser-source (ELS) assembly, optical
alignment and test with the silicon-photonics engine. NVIDIA separately lists
Browave, Corning, SENKO, TFC Communication and Coherent as connector/fibre
assembly experts, and Foxconn and Fabrinet as system-level CPO-assembly/test
and chassis-integration partners.

## Evidence boundary

This is a first-party ecosystem role map at NVIDIA's silicon-photonics CPO
platform boundary. It does **not** name SN6800/SN6810, a customer, a qualified
supplier allocation, sole-source status, a bill of materials, unit volumes,
price, yield, rework, warranty, capacity allocation, revenue or margin. The
ELS vendor statement is a shared process-role disclosure, not evidence that
any named company supplies the laser die, the entire ELS module, or a defined
share of Spectrum-X Ethernet CPO content.

## Research use

Use to improve the physical responsibility map and to identify diligence
targets for ELS, connector/fibre and system-integration processes. Do not use
to populate supplier-specific economics or to clear the exact-SKU commercial
proof gate.
