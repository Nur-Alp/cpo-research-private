# CMP-057 — Dell PowerSwitch SN6000 CPO route: evidence note

**Canonical catalogue:** [Dell AI Networking Switches](https://www.dell.com/en-us/shop/ai-switches/sf/ai-switches)  
**Supporting datasheet:** [Dell PowerSwitch SN6000 Ethernet Switch Series](https://www.delltechnologies.com/asset/en-us/products/networking/technical-support/dell-powerswitch-sn6000-series-spec-sheet.pdf)  
**Publisher:** Dell Technologies  
**Accessed and retained:** 11 August 2026  
**Local copies:** `CMP-057-dell-ai-switches-cpo-route.html`; `CMP-057-dell-powerswitch-sn6000-spec-sheet.pdf`  
**Source class:** Official OEM product catalogue and datasheet.

## What the source establishes

- Dell lists **PowerSwitch SN6800-LD** and **PowerSwitch SN6810-LD** as new NVIDIA Spectrum-6 AI switches with “Contact Sales” calls to action.
- The catalogue identifies **512 × MMC-12** for SN6800-LD and **128 × MMC-12** for SN6810-LD; in the same product table, the pluggable SN6600-LD is listed separately with **64 × OSFP224**.
- Dell’s datasheet calls SN6800-LD a four-ASIC co-packaged Ethernet switch and states that SN6810-LD uses silicon-photonics CPO. It describes an external laser source feeding integrated optical engines.

## Permitted use

This is independent OEM product-catalogue evidence that the NVIDIA CPO SKUs are being carried as Dell PowerSwitch products and remain physically distinct from Dell’s pluggable SN6600-LD configuration. It strengthens the OEM/channel and SKU-boundary record.

## Boundary and exclusions

“Contact Sales” and a product datasheet do **not** establish a booked order, delivery, customer acceptance, installed units/ports, repeat shipments, service history, supplier allocation, final-engine yield, warranty reserve, ASP or margin. The catalogue’s product availability is not a customer deployment numerator. Dell’s 5× power/reliability descriptions are vendor/product marketing claims, not matched system measurements.

## Research consequence

Record Dell as a named OEM/channel route for SN6810-LD/SN6800-LD. Keep commercial proof **open** until a customer or Dell record identifies an accepted CPO configuration, units and repeat deployment at the same SKU boundary.
