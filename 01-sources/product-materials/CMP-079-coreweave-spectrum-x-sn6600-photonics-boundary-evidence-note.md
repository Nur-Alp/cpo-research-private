# CMP-079 — CoreWeave Spectrum-X SN6600-LD / Photonics boundary

**Source type:** Official customer/operator blog
**Organisation:** CoreWeave
**Reviewed:** 2026-08-12
**Canonical URL:** https://coreweave.com/blog/why-ai-factories-need-proof-before-production
**Decision use:** NVIDIA exact-SKU commercial-proof negative control

## Evidence retained

CoreWeave says it was among the first cloud providers to deploy NVIDIA
Spectrum-X Ethernet **SN6600-LD**, described as a 102.4-Tb/s liquid-cooled
Ethernet switch. The same page separately describes CoreWeave as an early
adopter of NVIDIA Photonics co-packaged-optics networking.

## Boundary and limitation

The record does not say that the deployed SN6600-LD is an `SN6810-LD` or
`SN6800-LD`, nor does it state that the deployed SN6600-LD is the CPO
configuration. The page therefore cannot be used as exact-SKU Spectrum-X CPO
acceptance, unit/port volume, repeat shipment, field-service or supplier-
economics evidence. The two statements must not be joined by implication.

## Decision classification

**Observed:** named operator, SN6600-LD deployment, Spectrum-X platform use,
and separate Photonics CPO-adopter language.

**Open:** exact CPO SKU, CPO-versus-pluggable configuration, accepted units or
ports, repeat deployment, service record and economics.

Related claim: `CLM-548`.
