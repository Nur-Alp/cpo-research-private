# CMP-034 — Corning GlassBridge fiber-to-PIC connector platform

Canonical publisher page: <https://www.corning.com/oem-solutions/worldwide/en/home/products-solutions/optical-communication-components/next-generation-optics/glassbridge-connector.html>

Reviewed 2026-08-07. Corning describes GlassBridge as a wafer-based fiber-to-PIC platform using passive alignment and glass ion-exchange waveguides. The page positions it for NPO, CPO and high-density photonic modules; describes a rematable TMT physical-contact interface; and states that the detachable connector supports more than 24 optical channels in a compact form factor. Corning also presents the platform as supporting testing, rework and system flexibility.

Evidence boundary: this is a company product/platform description. It does not disclose insertion-loss distributions, channel-to-channel variation, mating-cycle results, environmental qualification, wafer yield, attach cycle time, rework cost, customer qualification, production volume, ASP or margin. The claimed manufacturability and serviceability are hypotheses to test against PAP-017, PAP-018, PAP-028 and production records.
