# CMP-035 — Intel OCI chiplet demonstration and silicon-photonics platform

- **Publisher:** Intel Newsroom / Intel Silicon Photonics
- **Canonical sources:** https://newsroom.intel.com/artificial-intelligence/intel-unveils-first-integrated-optical-io-chiplet and https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/products/details/network-io/silicon-photonics.html
- **Publication:** 2024 demonstration; product page accessed 2026-08-07
- **Evidence class:** Official company demonstration and product-platform page

Intel reports a fully integrated optical compute interconnect (OCI) chiplet co-packaged with an Intel CPU and running live data at OFC 2024. The reported first implementation supports 4 Tb/s bidirectional transfer, 64 channels of 32 Gb/s per direction, up to 100 m of standard single-mode fibre, and a stated 5 pJ/bit comparison against roughly 15 pJ/bit for pluggable optics. Intel says the chiplet integrates a silicon-photonics PIC with on-chip DWDM lasers and optical amplifiers and an electrical IC; 200G/lane PICs for 800G and 1.6T applications were under development.

Intel also reports more than 8 million PICs and more than 32 million integrated lasers shipped in pluggable transceivers, and says the OCI chiplet itself was a prototype being evaluated with select customers.

**Evidence boundary:** This is strong evidence for Intel's PIC integration route and prior volume experience in pluggable silicon photonics. It is not evidence of a production OCI/CPO chiplet, final-engine yield, fibre-attach cycle time, customer qualification, field reliability, ASP or margin. The 5 pJ/bit comparison is a company-reported demonstration comparison with a different product boundary and should not be inserted directly into the matched CPO/LPO TCO model.
