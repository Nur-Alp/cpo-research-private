# CMP-070 — Micas/Broadcom CPO component qualification dashboard

- **Publisher:** Micas Networks
- **Canonical source:** https://micasnetworks.com/CPO
- **Publication:** retrieved 2026-08-12; page presents Broadcom-sourced qualification status
- **Evidence class:** Partner product/platform page; historical TH5 context

Micas' CPO page presents a Broadcom-sourced qualification dashboard in which
the pluggable laser source, photonic IC and optical connector are marked as
having completed and passed qualification tests. The page also describes the
Broadcom Bailly CPO route and references more than four million lasers shipped
since 2016.

**Evidence boundary:** The dashboard is useful as a historical component-level
qualification and service-boundary control for Broadcom's prior CPO ecosystem.
It does not identify the exact TH6 `BCM78919` configuration, lot/sample size,
qualification protocol, customer acceptance, unit volume, repeat shipment,
qualified supplier share, ASP, yield/rework, warranty or margin. “Qualified”
must not be transferred from the historical Bailly route to TH6 without a
TH6-specific record.
