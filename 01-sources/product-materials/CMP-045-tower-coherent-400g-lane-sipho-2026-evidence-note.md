# CMP-045 — Tower/Coherent 400G-per-lane silicon-photonics demonstration

- **Primary record:** [retained SEC HTML](CMP-045-tower-coherent-400g-lane-sipho-2026.html) | [readable PDF snapshot](CMP-045-tower-coherent-400g-lane-sipho-2026.pdf) | [canonical SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/928876/000117891326001579/zk2634893.htm)
- **Date:** 23 March 2026.

## Evidence extracted

Tower Semiconductor and Coherent announced a **420 Gb/s PAM4 open-eye demonstration** using a silicon Mach–Zehnder modulator in what they describe as a production-ready silicon-photonics process. The demonstration used Coherent's InP continuous-wave high-power laser and targets next-generation 3.2T optical transceivers, with stated relevance to pluggable transceivers and CPO.

## Boundary and limitations

This is a component/modulator demonstration, not a complete 400G/lane optical engine or switch. The release does not disclose BER/FEC, TDECQ, reach, host electrical loss, temperature, optical power budget, sample count, yield, qualification, customer SKU, ASP or margin. “Production-ready” is a process description, not proof of production volume.

## Research use

Use CMP-045 as a primary 400G/lane silicon-modulator countercase: it strengthens the hypothesis that 400G may remain feasible in silicon-photonics/pluggable routes, but it does not clear the matched CPO/LPO/NPO system gate or establish Coherent/Tower profit capture.
