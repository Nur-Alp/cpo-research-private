## What the source establishes

Lumentum says NVIDIA entered a multiyear, nonexclusive agreement for advanced optics R&D, including a multibillion-dollar purchase commitment and future capacity access rights for advanced laser components. It also reports a $2 billion NVIDIA investment in Lumentum to support R&D, future capacity and operations, including a new U.S.-based fabrication facility.

## Evidence boundary

The release does not disclose a product SKU, CPO engine content, unit volume, ASP, margin, qualification result, customer deployment or cancellation protection. The agreement is a strong capacity and customer-route signal, not proof of delivered CPO revenue or complete-engine profit capture.
