# WHT-001 — Corning/Broadcom CPO fiber infrastructure white paper

Canonical publisher PDF: <https://www.corning.com/content/dam/corning/catalog/coc/documents/white-papers/LAN-3366-AEN.pdf>

Corning's publisher page and the joint white-paper record were reviewed on 2026-08-07. The paper covers silica single-mode and polarization-maintaining fibers in CPO switching environments, where dense routing, tight bends, thermal exposure and assembly handling create system-level reliability risks. It describes bend-radius-dependent fiber strength/failure modeling, the aggregate effect of high fiber counts, ribbon slack-management and bend-control hardware, and pre-assembled “known-good fiber” cassettes intended to reduce box-level handling damage.

Evidence boundary: this is a supplier-authored design and reliability white paper, not an independent field-failure dataset or a qualified CPO product report. The paper does not provide a Broadcom switch fleet denominator, final-engine yield, production attach cycle time, MTTR, warranty cost, or customer failure distribution. Its mechanisms are useful for the fibre-routing, assembly-control and service-cost gates; its modeled failure probabilities must not be entered as observed CPO failure rates.
