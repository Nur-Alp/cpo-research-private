# PRI-030 — TSMC 2026 COUPE-on-Substrate Milestone

- **Publisher:** Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company
- **Publication:** 22 April 2026
- **Canonical source:** [TSMC Debuts A13 Technology at 2026 North America Technology Symposium](https://pr.tsmc.com/english/news/3302)
- **Review status:** Read on 2026-08-07

## What it establishes

TSMC says its COUPE-on-substrate true CPO solution is beginning production in 2026. The same announcement describes a 200-Gb/s micro-ring-modulator implementation and claims 2× power efficiency and 10× latency reduction versus a pluggable version on the circuit board.

TSMC’s public HPC connectivity page separately reports 200-Gb/s optical modulation and greater than 99% 3D-stacking yield on engineering samples.

## Boundary

The production statement is a company milestone, not proof of shipped volume or a named customer SKU. The >99% figure is an engineering-sample 3D-stacking yield, not final optical-engine yield; it excludes PIC/laser selection, fibre attach, package, optical/electrical test, rework, customer acceptance and field reliability. The power and latency comparisons have no published matched system boundary in the announcement.
