# PRI-034 — Coherent OFC 2026 CPO demonstrations

- **Primary record:** [local HTML](PRI-034-coherent-ofc-2026-cpo.html) | [canonical Coherent release](https://www.coherent.com/news/press-releases/coherent-co-packaged-optics-cpo-technologies-ofc-2026)
- **Date:** 17 March 2026.

## Evidence

Coherent announced demonstrations of a **6.4T (32×200G) socketed silicon-photonics CPO** paired with its external laser source and high-power InP CW lasers, a VCSEL-based multimode socketed CPO, and an InP modulator on silicon operating at **400G per lane**.

## Boundary

These are demonstrations and a stated technology portfolio. The release does not provide complete-engine power, production yield, qualification, shipped units, customer identity, ASP, margin or service economics.
