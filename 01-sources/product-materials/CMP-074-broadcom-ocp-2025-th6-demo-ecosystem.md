# CMP-074 — Broadcom OCP 2025 TH6-Davisson demo ecosystem

- **Publisher:** Broadcom
- **Canonical source:** https://investors.broadcom.com/node/63621/pdf
- **Publication:** 2025-10-08
- **Evidence class:** Official partner-demo announcement; not a customer acceptance or shipment record

Broadcom's OCP Global Summit announcement says that Alpha Networks, Celestica,
DNI Emerging Technologies, Micas Networks and other partners would showcase
Tomahawk 6 or TH6-Davisson-related systems and demonstrations. It specifically
describes Micas as showing a TH6-Davisson CPO demo and names several Tomahawk-6
platform displays.

**Evidence boundary:** The announcement is a planned conference-showcase
record. It does not identify a purchased or accepted `BCM78919` system, an
end-customer operator, shipment or qualification date, accepted units/ports,
repeat delivery, field population, supplier allocation, ASP, yield, warranty
or margin.

**Decision treatment:** Retain as a stronger partner/demo route and a
false-positive control. A live or scheduled TH6 demonstration cannot be used
as a TH6 commercial numerator or evidence of repeatability.
