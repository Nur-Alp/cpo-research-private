# Coherent NVIDIA investment and CPO collaboration — SEC 8-K review

- **Registrant:** Coherent Corp.
- **Filing date:** 2 March 2026
- **Retained filing:** [SEC Form 8-K archive](PRI-026-coherent-nvidia-8k.html)
- **Canonical source:** <https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/820318/000119312526084366/d42735d8k.htm>
- **Reviewed:** 7 August 2026

## What the filing establishes

Coherent reports that it completed a private placement of 7,788,161 common shares to NVIDIA at $256.80 per share for aggregate cash proceeds of $2 billion. The filing says the investment supports R&D, future capacity expansion and operations. It also says the furnished collaboration gives NVIDIA access to five additional Coherent product families related to co-packaged optics.

## Evidence boundary

The executed financing and access arrangement are stronger than a press-release-only commitment. The filing still does not identify product-level purchase quantities, CPO SKU allocation, qualified output, ASP, margin, customer deployment or cancellation protection.

## Research use

Treat the $2B investment and five-product-family access as executed strategic/customer-route evidence. Do not treat them as CPO revenue or complete-engine supplier share.
