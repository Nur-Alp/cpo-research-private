## What it establishes

TSMC describes COUPE as a 3D photonics-stacking technology integrating a silicon-photonics chip and an electrical-control chip into a single photonic engine. The report says COUPE achieved 200-Gb/s high-speed data transmission with several customers in 2025, while TSMC continued work on CPO solutions targeting volume production in 2026. It separately says the COUPE technology service, using TSMC-SoIC chip-on-wafer stacking, was on track for development with volume production expected in 2026.

## Boundary

This is stronger than a generic roadmap because it includes a customer-linked 200-Gb/s result and a stated production target. It still does not identify customers, product SKUs, units, CPO allocation, complete-engine responsibility, final yield, qualification, ASP or margin. “Several customers” is not a shipment denominator, and the production target is not proof of volume output.
