# CMP-042 — POET Q1 2026 MD&A: optical-engine manufacturing and qualification boundary

- **Primary record:** [retained SEC HTML](CMP-042-poet-2026-20f.html) | [canonical SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1437424/000149315226023471/ex99-2.htm)
- **Issuer:** POET Technologies Inc.
- **Filing date:** 14 May 2026.

## Evidence extracted

POET describes its Optical Interposer as a wafer-level integration platform for optical engines and light sources. The filing says POET's Malaysia manufacturing partners, Globetronics and NationGate, support optical-engine assembly and test; a roughly 10,000-square-foot Globetronics cleanroom has installed wafer-level processing equipment with stated capacity of **1 million optical engines annually**. It also says equipment was transferred out of the former China joint-venture operation.

The filing reports that POET designed, tested and sampled a first-generation 400G transmit engine in 2024; its 800G receive engine was described as fully qualified and designed into several customers' optical-transceiver modules during 2024–2025; and second-generation 400G/800G transmit and 1.6T transmit/receive chipsets were sampled in 2025. It reports approximately **$15.9 million** of 2025 development investment in 800G/1.6T engines and light sources, with an additional **$30 million** expected over the following two years.

## Boundary and limitations

The capacity, qualification and customer-design-in claims are issuer disclosures. The filing does not provide engine-lot yield, first-pass attach yield, automated cycle time, Cpk, ASP, customer names, shipment units, field returns, warranty allocation or product margin. The 1-million-engine figure is installed line capacity, not output or demand.

## Research use

Use this record as an emerging optical-engine manufacturing comparator and as a reminder to separate installed capacity, customer qualification and production output. Do not treat POET's capacity or 800G qualification statement as evidence of CPO volume or profit-pool leadership.
