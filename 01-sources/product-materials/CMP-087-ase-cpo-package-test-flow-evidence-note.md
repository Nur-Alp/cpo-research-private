- **Reviewed:** 2026-08-13

## Bounded extraction

ASE describes a CPO flow that integrates laser, optics, FAU, PIC, EIC and other
ICs in one package, followed by final test. It identifies EIC/PIC 3D
integration and fibre assembly as key processes. It contrasts edge coupling
with active alignment against a wafer-level passive mirror-coupler route which
it presents as potentially better suited to high-volume testability. ASE also
lists wafer-level optical probing for known-good SiPh PIC die, high-accuracy
laser bonding, advanced 2.5D/3D packaging and multi-chip-module assembly.

## Permitted use

This is direct OSAT evidence of the full assembly/test sequence and an explicit
trade-off between optical performance and wafer-scale testability. It is a
process-map source, not a measured manufacturing outcome.

## Not permitted

No customer, CPO SKU, stage yield, test seconds, escape rate, rework fraction,
accepted-engine output, cost, warranty, revenue or margin is reported.
