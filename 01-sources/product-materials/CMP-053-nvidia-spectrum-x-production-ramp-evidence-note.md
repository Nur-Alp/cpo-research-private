## Evidence retained

NVIDIA states that Spectrum-X Ethernet Photonics is in full production and identifies a manufacturing chain: TSMC for silicon-photonics fabrication, SPIL for chip-scale packaging/assembly/testing, TFC for laser-die packaging and validation, and Foxconn for rack-ready system assembly. The same page names CoreWeave, Lambda and Oracle Cloud Infrastructure among first adopters and says systems are validated before customer shipment.

## Evidence boundary

This is a first-party production-ramp and supply-chain disclosure. It does not provide a CPO SKU/unit numerator, customer acceptance date, repeat shipment count, final optical-engine yield, test/rework data, field failure rate, supplier allocation, ASP, gross margin, warranty reserve or supplier revenue. “Full production” and pre-shipment validation therefore upgrade timing and responsibility mapping, not the commercial-proof or profit-pool gates.

## Research use

Use with the NVIDIA platform dossier, the CPO content-attribution map, the customer-proof register and the cost-per-qualified-good-engine gate. Do not attribute the named TSMC, SPIL, TFC or Foxconn roles to a specific supplier margin without a product BOM or contract disclosure.
