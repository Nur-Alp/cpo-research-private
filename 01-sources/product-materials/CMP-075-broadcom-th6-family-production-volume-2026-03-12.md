# CMP-075 — Broadcom Tomahawk 6 production-volume shipping update

**Canonical source:** https://investors.broadcom.com/news-releases/news-release-details/broadcom-now-shipping-worlds-first-1024-tbps-switch-production
**PDF:** https://investors.broadcom.com/node/64031/pdf
**Reviewed:** 2026-08-12

Broadcom's March 12, 2026 release states that the Tomahawk 6 **family** is
shipping in production volume and moved from initial samples to production
deployment. It describes support for 100G and 200G SerDes and multiple
interconnect options.

This is not an exact TH6-Davisson/BCM78919 CPO shipment record. The release
does not identify a customer, CPO-versus-copper configuration, accepted units,
repeat deliveries, supplier allocation, yield, ASP, margin, field service or
warranty. Record it as family-level timing evidence only.
