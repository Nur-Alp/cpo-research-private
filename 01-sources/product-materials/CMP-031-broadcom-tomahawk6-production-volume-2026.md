# CMP-020 — Broadcom Tomahawk 6 production-volume announcement

Canonical publisher page: <https://investors.broadcom.com/news-releases/news-release-details/broadcom-now-shipping-worlds-first-1024-tbps-switch-production>

Reviewed 2026-08-07. The March 12, 2026 release says the Tomahawk 6 family is “now shipping in production volume,” moved from initial samples to production deployment in less than three quarters, supports 100G and 200G SerDes, and is designed for scale-out and scale-up AI networks. The release describes the switch family, not a separately identified CPO SKU. It does not disclose CPO unit volume, customer identities, optical-engine suppliers, final-engine yield, qualification, ASP, margin, or the share of shipments using CPO versus copper or pluggable optics.

Evidence boundary: this is strong evidence for Broadcom switch-platform production status, but not independent proof of CPO production volume or optical-engine profit capture. Keep the CPO configuration denominator open.
