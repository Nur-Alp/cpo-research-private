# CMP-080 — Aehr August 2026 silicon-photonics burn-in production order

**Canonical source:** [Aehr Test Systems — production order from lead silicon-photonics customer](https://www.aehr.com/2026/08/aehr-test-systems-expands-silicon-photonics-production-momentum-with-follow-on-production-order-from-lead-customer/)  
**Publisher:** Aehr Test Systems  
**Publication date:** 2026-08-04  
**Accessed:** 2026-08-12  
**Local preservation copy:** `CMP-080-aehr-august-2026-follow-on-sipho-burn-in-order.html`

## What the source says

- Aehr reports a follow-on production order from its lead silicon-photonics
  wafer-level burn-in customer for a fully automated FOX-XP multi-wafer
  production burn-in system.
- The system has nine independent WaferPak test blades, each rated for up to
  3,500 W, with automated wafer handling and loading/unloading.
- Aehr frames the customer application broadly across silicon-photonic
  integrated circuits used in optical transceivers and emerging chip-to-chip
  optical-I/O architectures, and discusses optical switching, CPO and LPO as
  industry applications.
- The announcement describes continued expansion of the customer's
  manufacturing capacity and positions wafer-level burn-in as a production
  reliability-screening step before packaging.

## Boundary and limitations

This is stronger than a generic equipment roadmap because it is a follow-on
production order and identifies an expanding customer manufacturing program.
However, the customer, optical product, exact CPO architecture, SKU, wafer
starts, accepted die/engine output, burn-in duration, test coverage, pass/fail
distribution, utilisation, price and margin are not disclosed.

The source therefore **does not establish**:

- NVIDIA `SN6800-LD`/`SN6810-LD` or Broadcom `BCM78919` involvement;
- switch-CPO deployment, customer acceptance or repeat CPO shipments;
- wafer-to-engine or final-engine yield;
- burn-in cost per accepted engine, field reliability, warranty or supplier
  economics.

## Controlled use

Use this record to upgrade the manufacturing diligence map from “equipment
capability/order” to “production-scale SiPh screening demand.” Keep all
product-matched yield, accepted-unit, supplier-share and profit-pool fields
blocked. Do not join this unnamed customer to any NVIDIA, Broadcom, Coherent,
Lumentum, TSMC or Marvell product without a separate source.
