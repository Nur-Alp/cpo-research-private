# PRI-031 — TSMC COUPE Research Description

- **Publisher:** TSMC Research
- **Canonical source:** [Heterogeneous Integration of a Compact Universal Photonic Engine for Silicon Photonics Applications in HPC](https://research.tsmc.com/page/interconnect/30.html)
- **Review status:** Read on 2026-08-07

## What it establishes

TSMC describes COUPE as an EIC–PIC integration platform designed to minimize electrical coupling loss. It says the structure is intended to provide low insertion loss for both grating- and edge-coupler implementations, avoids cavities or mechanically weak parts, and can integrate with a host ASIC to form a co-package. The page frames the paper as a proposed integration platform responding to the lack of a common SiPh solution.

## Boundary

This is an architecture description and paper pointer, not a current product specification. The page supplies no numeric loss distribution, 200G/400G end-to-end link, production lot, final yield, qualification, customer or economics. “Low insertion loss” must not be substituted for a measured production margin.
