# CMP-038 — Acacia 200G/lane silicon-photonic optical-engine family

- **Publisher:** Acacia (Cisco)
- **Canonical source:** https://acacia-inc.com/blog/acacia-expands-client-optics-component-business/
- **Publication:** 2025-03-25
- **Evidence class:** Official product announcement

Acacia announced a family of 200G-per-lane silicon-photonic optical engines alongside the 3 nm Kibo 1.6T PAM4 DSP. Acacia states that the portfolio targets 1.6T and 800G client-optics configurations, including retimed-optics and diagnostic/loopback use cases, and that the Kibo DSP was expected to sample later in 2025. The announcement also says Acacia had shipped more than one million 100G-per-lane optical engines in the prior twelve months.

**Evidence boundary:** This is directly relevant to the scale-out engine/PIC supplier universe and establishes a product roadmap plus prior engine volume claim. It does not establish 200G/lane production shipment, final-engine yield, qualification, fibre-attach loss, power under a matched boundary, ASP, gross margin or customer-specific CPO content. The prior-volume claim is for 100G-per-lane engines and should not be transferred to 200G/lane products.
