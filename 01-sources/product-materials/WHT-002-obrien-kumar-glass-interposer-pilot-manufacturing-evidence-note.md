# WHT-002 — O’Brien and Kumar, “Co-packaging of photonic and electronic ICs on glass interposers”

**Canonical source:** <https://chipscalereview.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Chip-Scale-Review_Mar-Apr_2025-digital.pdf>  
**Publisher issue:** *Chip Scale Review*, March–April 2025, Vol. 29 No. 2  
**Authors:** Peter O’Brien and Das Kumar, Tyndall National Institute  
**Local preservation:** Full issue download was blocked by the publisher/CDN in this environment; retain the canonical PDF link above. The article is also indexed in the publisher search record.

## Evidence extracted

- The article frames glass interposers as a route for co-packaging PICs and EICs using through-glass vias (TGVs), with the objective of moving from early research toward scalable, cost-efficient manufacturing.
- It identifies glass properties relevant to CPO: low RF loss, thermal stability, a coefficient of thermal expansion comparable to silicon, dimensional stability and flat bonding surfaces that may improve yield.
- It describes LPKF laser-induced deep etching (LIDE) as a two-step process—laser modification followed by wet chemical etching—for high-aspect-ratio glass structures without the micro-cracks/chipping associated with less controlled processing; the indexed article text says features as small as approximately 5 µm can be produced.
- The article states that TGVs can materially increase local thermal conductivity and that panel-level glass processing could improve scaling and cost versus wafer-only processing.
- It explicitly treats automation, precision assembly and standardized design/assembly processes as prerequisites for high-volume production.

## Boundary and limitations

This is a technical trade-publication article, not a disclosed production-line qualification report. The indexed record does not provide a complete CPO engine, lot-level yield, Cpk, cycle time, rework, field reliability, customer SKU, ASP or margin. Numerical material-property values and LIDE capabilities should be traced to the article’s cited primary references before entering a quantitative model.

## Research use

Use WHT-002 to expand the pilot-manufacturing and glass-interposer equipment diligence queue, alongside `PAP-032`, `PAP-043`, `PAP-044` and the glass test-vehicle work. Do not treat it as proof that glass/TGV is the lowest-cost or highest-yield commercial route.
