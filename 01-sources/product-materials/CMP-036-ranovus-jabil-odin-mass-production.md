# CMP-036 — Ranovus/Jabil ODIN optical-engine manufacturing collaboration

- **Publisher:** Ranovus
- **Canonical source:** https://ranovus.com/ranovus-collaborates-with-jabil/
- **Publication:** 2025-03-31
- **Evidence class:** Official manufacturing collaboration announcement

Ranovus announced a strategic manufacturing collaboration with Jabil to enable high-volume production of the ODIN optical engine. Ranovus describes ODIN as a monolithic electronic-photonic integrated circuit (EPIC) that integrates lasers, modulators, photodetectors, drivers, transimpedance amplifiers and control loops. The announcement positions ODIN in CPO and NPO implementations, including 800G and 6.4T configurations, for AI and data-center interconnects.

**Evidence boundary:** The announcement is relevant to the PIC/engine ownership and outsourced-manufacturing route. “Enable high-volume production” is a planned manufacturing objective, not evidence of shipped production volume, qualified final-engine yield, attach/test cycle time, customer SKU, ASP, gross margin or field reliability. No customer, unit count, line qualification or Jabil content share is disclosed.
