# CMP-022 — Lumentum Greensboro InP laser facility

Canonical publisher page: <https://investor.lumentum.com/financial-news-releases/news-details/2026/Lumentum-Announces-New-U-S--Manufacturing-Facility-to-Produce-Advanced-Lasers-for-the-Worlds-Largest-AI-Data-Centers/default.aspx>

Reviewed 2026-08-07. Lumentum announced a 240,000-square-foot Greensboro, North Carolina facility for InP-based CW and UHP lasers. The release says NVIDIA will be a customer, the site will use 6-inch InP wafers, production is expected to ramp in mid-2028, and Lumentum plans to invest hundreds of millions of dollars while preserving or creating more than 400 U.S. manufacturing jobs.

Evidence boundary: this is a dated capacity and customer-route milestone for the laser layer. It does not disclose wafer starts, good-device yield, qualified output, product mix, ASP, margin, CPO-engine content, or shipped volume. Treat mid-2028 as a checkpoint, not a forecast of CPO deployment.
