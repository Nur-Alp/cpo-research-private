# CMP-043 — POET/Lumilens wafer-level photonic integration agreement

- **Primary record:** [retained SEC HTML](CMP-043-poet-lumilens-2026-supply-agreement.html) | [readable PDF snapshot](CMP-043-poet-lumilens-2026-supply-agreement.pdf) | [canonical SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1437424/000117184326003413/exh_991.htm)
- **Date:** 14 May 2026.

## Evidence extracted

POET and Lumilens announced a joint development and commercial technology partnership around an Electrical-Optical Interposer (EOI). The release describes active-alignment-free wafer-level optical-engine production as intended to reduce cost, yield and throughput constraints. It reports an initial Lumilens purchase order valued at **$50 million** and a framework that could scale to **more than $500 million** of cumulative purchases over five years.

The release expects engineering samples in late 2026 and production ramp aligned to hyperscaler deployments in 2027. It explicitly makes fulfilment and revenue conditional on successful module development, ultimate qualification and manufacturing scale-up.

## Boundary and limitations

The purchase order, roadmap and manufacturing claims are first-party transaction disclosures, not shipped revenue or proof of qualified CPO engines. The release does not name an end hyperscaler, specify the optical-engine SKU, disclose units, ASP, gross margin, yield or final package responsibility.

## Research use

Use CMP-043 as a commercial-intent and qualification-gated comparator. Keep the order value separate from recognized revenue and do not infer that POET or Lumilens controls the complete CPO profit pool.
