# CMP-037 — Cisco CPO architecture and supply-chain qualification boundary

- **Publisher:** Cisco Blogs
- **Canonical source:** https://blogs.cisco.com/sp/transforming-infrastructure-for-the-ai-era
- **Publication:** 2025 (accessed 2026-08-07)
- **Evidence class:** Official company technical strategy post

Cisco reports a 51.2T switch demonstration populated with 64 × 800G linear pluggable optics and says the architecture reduced system power by 30% versus traditional retimed optics. Cisco also says it continues to work on CPO, following capability demonstrations in 2023, and that its approach uses multiple sources for silicon-photonics wafer fabs, DSPs, lasers and manufacturing across geographies. The post states that qualification is performed at both transceiver-module and fully loaded platform levels.

**Evidence boundary:** This is useful evidence for Cisco's system-level comparator and its stated multi-source qualification/supply-chain approach. It does not provide a controlled CPO-versus-LPO measurement, production CPO shipment, PIC/engine supplier allocation, final-engine yield, field reliability, ASP or margin. The 30% figure is a Cisco-reported comparison for the demonstrated linear-pluggable system and must not be generalized to CPO.
