# CMP-077 — Lambda CPO networking adoption statement

**Canonical source:** https://lambda.ai/blog/silicon-photonics-for-ai-clusters-performance
**Reviewed:** 2026-08-12

Lambda says it is preparing next-generation GPU clusters to integrate NVIDIA
Quantum-X InfiniBand Photonics and Spectrum-X Photonics Ethernet switches,
including GB300 NVL72 and Vera Rubin NVL144 cluster plans.

This is customer/operator adoption and planning evidence, not a delivered
Spectrum-X Ethernet SKU record. Lambda's separate exact unboxing record is for
Quantum-X InfiniBand Q3450-LD. No Spectrum-X SN6800/SN6810-LD acceptance,
units/ports, repeat shipment, field service, supplier allocation or economics
are disclosed.
