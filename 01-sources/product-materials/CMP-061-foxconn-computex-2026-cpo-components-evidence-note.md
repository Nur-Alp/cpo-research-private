# CMP-061 — Foxconn COMPUTEX 2026 CPO component display

**Canonical source:** <https://www.foxconn.com/en-us/press-center/press-releases/latest-news/2044>  
**Publisher:** Hon Hai Technology Group (Foxconn)  
**Publication date:** 1 June 2026  
**Local file:** [original official web record](CMP-061-foxconn-computex-2026-cpo-components.html)

## Evidence extracted

Foxconn’s COMPUTEX 2026 release says it displayed CPO, 1.6T optical-communication modules, CPC Cable to XPO, and ELSFP for a CPO-switch solution. The same passage describes L1–L12 manufacturing hardware and frames the display as evidence of vertical integration in AI servers and next-generation data-centre architecture.

## Correct use

This is first-party **component-capability and product-boundary evidence**. It establishes that Foxconn publicly presented an ELSFP-for-CPO-switch solution alongside CPO/interconnect components. It can be used to identify Foxconn/FIT as a diligence target for the external-light, cable/connector and integration layers.

## Limitations

- A trade-show display is not a product shipment, customer qualification, production order or accepted deployment.
- The release does not name a switch ASIC, PIC, optical engine, product SKU, customer, supplier relationship, bill of materials, qualified share, yield, test process, ASP, margin or warranty responsibility.
- It must not be used to attach Foxconn’s generic CPO capability to NVIDIA Spectrum-X or Broadcom TH6-Davisson, nor to infer a complete-engine role.

**Use:** supplier-content diligence lead only. See `CLM-528`.
