## Evidence boundary

This recovers the underlying first-party briefing behind the earlier
secondary-source observation. It supports a bounded historical **TH5-Bailly**
qualification/lab-reliability context. The PDF does not disclose population,
customer, operating site, elapsed time, test conditions, censoring, confidence
interval, link/port denominator, field returns, warranty cost, or the
configuration of the comparator systems.

The slides do **not** establish TH6-Davisson customer acceptance, TH6 shipped
units, TH6 field reliability, 200G/lane reliability, CPO economics, or a
universal CPO-versus-LPO power/reliability result. The 65% / 35% power figures
are explicitly 100G/lane TH5 claims and must remain separate from the
project's matched-boundary architecture work.
