# CMP-050 — Lightmatter vClick detachable FAU

**Canonical source:** <https://lightmatter.co/press-release/lightmatter-unveils-vclick-optics-industry-first-detachable-fiber-array-unit-for-cpo-advanced-packaging-and-high-volume-production/>  
**Publisher:** Lightmatter  
**Publication date:** 2026-03-11  
**Local files:** [HTML snapshot](CMP-050-lightmatter-vclick-detachable-fau.html) · [readable PDF snapshot](CMP-050-lightmatter-vclick-detachable-fau.pdf)

## Evidence extracted

- Lightmatter announces vClick Optics, a detachable fibre-array unit integrated with SENKO's SEAT/MPC connector technologies for its Passage 3D photonic interconnects.
- The release reports insertion and re-insertion loss below **1.5 dB** and positions the interface for 32–100 Tbps+ optical connectivity.
- It says the interface is compatible with mold-and-grind advanced-packaging flows and is designed to avoid active fibre alignment during production.
- The stated manufacturing concept is to verify “known good optical engines” before final integration with high-value ASIC/XPU package structures, shifting testing earlier in the flow.
- The stated serviceability concept is a detachable FAU rather than permanent fibre attachment, allowing field replacement of the fibre interface.

## What this changes

This is a current first-party product-route signal for the known-good-engine and serviceability hypotheses. It directly supports the manufacturing gate's requirement to separate optical-engine test from expensive final ASIC integration. It does not establish that vClick has shipped in volume or that its claimed loss and serviceability translate into lower cost per qualified engine.

## Limitations

- Company announcement and partner statements; no independent production-lot data.
- No sample count, loss distribution, mating-cycle life, environmental qualification, first-pass yield, cycle time, ASP, customer SKU or field-return data.
- “High-volume manufacturing” and “hyperscale volume-ready” are product positioning claims, not evidence of shipped volume.

**Use:** detachable FAU / known-good optical-engine route; keep separate from customer production proof. See `CLM-401`–`CLM-405`.
