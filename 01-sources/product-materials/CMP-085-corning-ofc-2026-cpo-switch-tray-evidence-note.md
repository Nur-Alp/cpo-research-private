## What the company states

Corning describes an OFC 2026 demonstration of a fully assembled CPO switch
tray with Nexthop. The company says the tray combines Broadcom silicon,
Corning fibre-array units (FAUs), and optical-management hardware as a
manufacturable complete solution.[CLM-565]

## Decision use

This is a supplier-authored physical-content and integration signal: it moves
Corning beyond a generic CPO-connectivity capability statement to a named
switch-tray demonstration involving Broadcom silicon and Nexthop. It is useful
for the fibre-array-unit/optical-management layer of the supplier map.
