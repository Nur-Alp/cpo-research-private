# PRI-001 — Teramount detachable CPO connector patent

- **Canonical record:** https://patents.google.com/patent/US20230130045A1/en
- **Publication:** US20230130045A1, *Detachable connector for co-packaged optics*
- **Assignee shown by public patent record:** Teramount Ltd
- **Published:** 2023-04-27
- **Reviewed:** 2026-08-13
- **Source class:** Public patent application / architecture disclosure

## Bounded extraction

The application describes a detachable photonic plug, a receptacle attached to
the PIC/multi-chip-module boundary, and a fibre ribbon held in V-grooves. The
plug is assembled before it is inserted into the receptacle; the disclosure
frames removability as useful for maintenance and long-term care.

## Permitted use

It demonstrates that a detachable PIC-to-fibre boundary is a concrete,
patented architecture with an explicit pre-assembly and mechanical-alignment
sequence. It supports a serviceability and attachment-architecture map only.

## Not permitted

Do not infer a commercial product, qualification result, mating-life
performance, attach yield, customer, exact CPO SKU, supplier allocation,
revenue, margin, or field-replacement cost from this patent.
