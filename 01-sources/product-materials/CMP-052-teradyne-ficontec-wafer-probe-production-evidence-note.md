# CMP-052 — Teradyne / ficonTEC production system for double-sided wafer-probe test

**Canonical source:** <https://investors.teradyne.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/9/teradyne-announces-production-system-for-double-sided-wafer-probe-test-for-silicon-photonics>  
**Date:** 31 March 2025  
**Local preservation:** [HTML archive](CMP-052-teradyne-ficontec-wafer-probe-production.html) · [readable PDF snapshot](CMP-052-teradyne-ficontec-wafer-probe-production.pdf)

## Evidence extracted

- Teradyne and ficonTEC announced availability of a high-volume, double-sided wafer-probe test cell for silicon photonics.
- The system combines Teradyne UltraFLEXplus ATE and IG-XL with ficonTEC optical alignment, probing and wafer-handling technology.
- The companies state that the system is intended to test hybrid-bonded PIC/EIC wafers in a production environment and to provide high-throughput electro-optical testing for CPO applications.
- Teradyne describes an open ecosystem rather than a mandatory single-vendor optical test stack.

## Boundary and limitations

This is an equipment-availability and production-intent announcement, not evidence that a customer has achieved a stated wafer yield, test time, Cpk, escape rate or final-engine cost. It does not identify a customer, installed base, throughput, qualification lot, ASP, equipment utilization or CPO revenue.
