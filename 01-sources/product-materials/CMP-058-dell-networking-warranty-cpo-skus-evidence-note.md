# CMP-058 — Dell warranty coverage for CPO SKU families: evidence note

**Canonical source:** [Dell Technologies ProSupport Enterprise Suite — Networking Warranty Coverage](https://www.dell.com/en-au/lp/networking-warranty)  
**Publisher:** Dell Technologies  
**Accessed and retained:** 11 August 2026  
**Local preservation copy:** `CMP-058-dell-networking-warranty-cpo-skus.html`  
**Source class:** Official OEM support-policy page.

## What the source says

- Dell’s SN-Series warranty table lists both **SN6800-LD** and **SN6810-LD** with a **limited three-year warranty**, **next-business-day onsite service**, and **90 days** for the listed software-support term.
- Dell defines next-business-day onsite service as repair/replacement performed by a Dell technician by the next business day, subject to the relevant service contract.
- The policy says repair/replacement can cover service parts and/or whole-unit exchange for defective hardware within the hardware-warranty period.

## Permitted use

This is a product-level OEM support-policy fact. It demonstrates that the NVIDIA CPO SKU families have an explicit system-level warranty/replacement channel, which is relevant to the service-boundary analysis.

## Boundary and exclusions

The source does **not** say whether a failed optical engine, external laser, fibre assembly, package or ASIC is repaired in situ or replaced as a unit. It does not disclose response achieved in practice, customer downtime, failure rates, MTTR, spares, warranty reserve, field-return cost, geography-specific terms, customer deployment, unit volume, product price, or supplier economic allocation.

## Research consequence

Do not describe CPO serviceability as unaddressed at the OEM-system level; describe it precisely as **a stated system warranty/replacement route with engine-level procedure and economics still open**.
