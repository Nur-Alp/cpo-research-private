## What the source establishes

Broadcom describes its second-generation Tomahawk 5-Bailly product as the industry's first volume-production CPO solution at 100G per lane. The release also records partner milestones: Micas says it launched a volume-production 100G/lane CPO system, while Delta announces production of a 51.2T TH5-Bailly CPO Ethernet switch. Foxconn reports production release of CPO LGA sockets and pluggable laser-source cages/connectors.

## Evidence boundary

This is primary vendor and partner reporting, not an independently audited unit count or field-reliability dataset. It establishes a dated 100G/lane production baseline. It does not prove 200G/lane TH6-Davisson volume, customer unit counts, repeat orders, complete-engine yield, ASP, margin or field service economics.

## Research use

Upgrade the 100G/lane switch-CPO maturity state while keeping the 200G/lane commercial gate separate. Use the partner milestones as production-route evidence, not as a 200G adoption numerator.
