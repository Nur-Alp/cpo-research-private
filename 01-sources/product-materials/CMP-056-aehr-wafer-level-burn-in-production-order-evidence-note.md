# CMP-056 — Aehr wafer-level burn-in production order: evidence note

**Canonical source:** [Aehr Receives Follow-On Order from Major Silicon Photonics Customer for Fully Automated Wafer-Level Burn-In System for Hyperscale Data Center Optical Interconnect](https://www.aehr.com/2026/06/aehr-receives-follow-on-order-from-major-silicon-photonics-customer-for-fully-automated-wafer-level-burn-in-system-for-hyperscale-data-center-optical-interconnect/)  
**Publisher:** Aehr Test Systems  
**Publication date:** 17 June 2026  
**Accessed and retained:** 11 August 2026  
**Local preservation copy:** `CMP-056-aehr-wafer-level-burn-in-production-order.html`  
**Source class:** Primary supplier press release; vendor-reported customer and production claim.

## What the source says

- Aehr says it received a **follow-on production order** for a fully automated FOX-XP wafer-level burn-in system from an unnamed customer it describes as a major supplier of networking products and data-centre optical transceivers.
- The described system tests **nine wafers in parallel**, includes automated alignment and wafer contactors, and is scheduled for delivery within six months.
- Aehr says the customer provided a forecast for additional systems during calendar 2026 as it ramps capacity for next-generation hyperscale deployments.
- Aehr says it had already delivered one full production system and two engineering systems by May after engagement began seven months earlier.

## Permitted use

This is credible evidence that an unnamed silicon-photonics supplier is investing in production-oriented wafer-level burn-in capacity for hyperscale optical interconnects. It supports the report's treatment of test, burn-in and known-good-die screening as manufacturing control points.

## Boundary and exclusions

The announcement **does not identify** the customer, the optical product, any CPO SKU, a switch platform, wafer starts, good-die yield, test seconds, coverage, burn-in duration, installed utilisation, engine output, customer acceptance, supplier share, ASP, gross margin or warranty cost. It is not evidence of switch-CPO deployment, repeat CPO shipment, final-engine yield, or Aehr CPO profit capture. Phrases such as “production ramp” and “volume production” are supplier statements and must remain attributed.

## Research consequence

Use only to elevate the diligence priority of wafer-level burn-in and throughput. Do not insert it into a cost-per-good-engine calculation or use it to rank PIC, engine, platform or test suppliers.
