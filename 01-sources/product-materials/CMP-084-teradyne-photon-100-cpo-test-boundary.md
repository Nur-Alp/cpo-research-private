# CMP-084 — Teradyne Photon 100 CPO test-insertion boundary

## Retained source

- **Publisher:** Teradyne
- **Title:** *Photon 100*
- **Canonical URL:** <https://www.teradyne.com/products/photon-100/>
- **Access checked:** 2026-08-13

## Evidence retained

Teradyne describes Photon 100 as an automated silicon-photonics test platform
for high-volume manufacturing environments. Its stated test insertions span
wafer test, optical-engine/package test, and co-packaged module test; its
listed applications include switches and AI accelerators with CPO. The platform
also describes production-level double-sided electro-optical test for
hybrid-bonded PIC/EIC wafers and an open test-cell ecosystem.

## Evidence boundary

This is a test-equipment capability and test-insertion map, not a record of an
installed line, named CPO customer, product cycle time, test seconds, coverage,
wafer/engine yield, false-reject/escape rate, rework rate, cost per good engine,
field reliability, or revenue. “High-volume” and “production-level” describe
the supplier's equipment positioning, not a verified output result for NVIDIA,
Broadcom or any other CPO product.

## Research use

Use to distinguish wafer-only test availability from the full three-stage
wafer → engine/package → CPO-module test requirement. Do not use it to populate
test cost, final yield, CPO capacity, supplier economics or commercial proof.
