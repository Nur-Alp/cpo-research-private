# Celestica DS6000-series 1.6TbE switches available to order

**Publication date:** 2026-04-29

## Evidence boundary

Celestica's official announcement describes DS6000-series switches as available for order to initial customers, with up to 102.4Tb/s capacity through 64 × 1.6TbE **OSFP224** ports and support for both high-speed copper and optical interconnects. Celestica identifies the platform as using Broadcom Tomahawk 6 switch silicon; it does not identify `BCM78919` or TH6-Davisson.

This is a route-to-market and alternative-architecture record. **OSFP224 is a pluggable form-factor interface**, so the named shipping/ordering platform is not evidence that the CPO-only `BCM78919` configuration was selected. The source does **not** identify a CPO-specific configuration, customer, shipment count, qualification state, optical-engine bill of materials, supplier allocation, ASP, margin or field performance. It must not be used as CPO shipment evidence.
