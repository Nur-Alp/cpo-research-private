# Lumentum NVIDIA investment and optics collaboration — SEC 8-K review

- **Registrant:** Lumentum Holdings Inc.
- **Filing date:** 2 March 2026
- **Retained filing:** [SEC Form 8-K archive](PRI-027-lumentum-nvidia-8k.html)
- **Canonical source:** <https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633978/000119312526085412/d41019d8k.htm>
- **Reviewed:** 7 August 2026

## What the filing establishes

Lumentum reports that it completed a private placement of 2,876,415 Series A convertible preferred shares to NVIDIA at $695.31 per share for aggregate cash proceeds of $2 billion. The filing furnishes the strategic agreement describing a multibillion-dollar purchase commitment and future capacity rights for advanced laser components.

## Evidence boundary

The executed financing is stronger than an announcement alone. The filing does not disclose product-level purchase quantities, CPO engine content, qualified output, ASP, margin, customer deployment or cancellation protection.

## Research use

Treat the $2B investment as executed strategic/capacity evidence for Lumentum's laser layer. Keep complete-engine supplier share and profit capture open.
