# CMP-086 — Advantest silicon-photonics / CPO test capability

- **Canonical record:** https://www.advantest.com/tw/products/semiconductor-test-system/soc/v93000/
- **Publisher:** Advantest
- **Reviewed:** 2026-08-13
- **Source class:** Supplier product-capability page

## Bounded extraction

Advantest describes production-oriented electro-optical test-cell concepts for
silicon photonics and CPO, spanning wafer-level validation through final test.
It identifies precise, stable and repeatable optical access at wafer test and
automated optical connections at final test as challenges. The page states that
the industry lacks a standardized optical package-fibre connector.

## Permitted use

This is direct supplier evidence that final-test connector handling and optical
access remain manufacturing boundaries, complementary to the Teradyne/ficonTEC
test-flow record.

## Not permitted

It does not identify a customer, installed line, CPO SKU, throughput, yield,
coverage, escape rate, cost, service record, revenue or margin.
