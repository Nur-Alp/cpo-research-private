# NVIDIA–Coherent strategic optics agreement — primary-source review

- **Publisher:** Coherent Corp.
- **Publication date:** 2 March 2026
- **Canonical source:** <https://www.coherent.com/news/press-releases/nvidia-and-coherent-announce-strategic-partnership>
- **Local retention:** [Publisher HTML archive](PRI-023-nvidia-coherent-strategic-partnership.html) retained and reviewed; canonical link remains authoritative.
- **Reviewed:** 7 August 2026

## What the source establishes

Coherent says NVIDIA entered a multiyear, nonexclusive strategic agreement covering advanced optics technology, manufacturing capacity and R&D. It reports a multibillion-dollar NVIDIA purchase commitment, future access and capacity rights for advanced laser and optical-networking products, and a $2 billion NVIDIA investment in Coherent to support R&D, future capacity and operations.

## Evidence boundary

The announcement does not identify product SKUs, CPO versus pluggable allocation, unit volumes, ASP, gross margin, customer pricing, cancellation terms, qualification status or revenue recognition. “Multibillion-dollar purchase commitment” is not equivalent to realised CPO revenue or a complete-engine award.

## Research use

Upgrade Coherent's capacity-access and customer-route evidence. Keep complete-engine supplier share, qualified yield and CPO-specific economics open.
