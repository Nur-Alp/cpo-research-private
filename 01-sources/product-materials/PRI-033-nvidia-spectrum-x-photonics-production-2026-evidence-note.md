# PRI-033 — NVIDIA Spectrum-X Ethernet Photonics production announcement

- **Primary record:** [local HTML](PRI-033-nvidia-spectrum-x-photonics-production-2026.html) | [canonical NVIDIA Newsroom page](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/vera-rubin-full-production-agentic-ai-factory)
- **Date:** 31 May 2026.

## Evidence

NVIDIA states that Spectrum-X Ethernet Photonics is a CPO-based switch with **200Gb/s SerDes** and is **now in production**. It names CoreWeave, Lambda and Oracle Cloud Infrastructure among first ecosystem partners/adopters and claims 5× power efficiency, 5× AI uptime and 1.3× faster deployment versus traditional transceivers.

## Boundary

This is a first-party production/adoption claim. It does not disclose switch units, optical-engine supplier allocation, final-engine yield, qualification lot, ASP, margin or CPO revenue. The performance comparisons are company-stated and lack a matched public test protocol.
