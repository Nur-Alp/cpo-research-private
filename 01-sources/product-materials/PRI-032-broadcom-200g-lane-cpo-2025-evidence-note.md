# PRI-032 — Broadcom third-generation 200G/lane CPO announcement

- **Primary record:** [local HTML](PRI-032-broadcom-200g-lane-cpo-2025.html) | [canonical Broadcom page](https://www.broadcom.com/company/news/product-releases/63126)
- **Date:** 15 May 2025.

## Evidence

Broadcom announced a third-generation **200G/lane CPO** product line and said its second-generation 100G/lane TH5-Bailly solution was the industry's first volume-production CPO solution. The announcement describes automated testing, OSAT processes, thermal designs, handling, fibre routing and yield as learning areas, and states a commitment to a fourth-generation 400G/lane solution.

## Boundary

This is a company product announcement. It establishes Broadcom's stated product and process roadmap, not independently verified shipment volume, final-engine yield, ASP, margin, customer units or field reliability.
