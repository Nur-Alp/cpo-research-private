# CMP-065 — Tower Semiconductor silicon-photonics contracts and capacity prepayments

**Canonical source:** [Tower Semiconductor Form 6-K and 13 May 2026 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/928876/000117891326002609/zk2635309.htm)  
**Publisher:** Tower Semiconductor  
**Publication date:** 13 May 2026  
**Access date:** 12 August 2026  
**Local raw archive:** Not retained. A direct scripted retrieval returned an SEC automated-access notice rather than the filing and was discarded.

## Evidence boundary

Search-indexed SEC text for the issuer's furnished release states that Tower signed
silicon-photonics customer contracts for **$1.3 billion of 2027 revenue** and
received **$290 million of customer prepayments** for capacity reservation. The
release says its silicon-photonics capability is intended to support both
high-volume pluggable transceivers and next-generation NPO/CPO solutions.

## Correct use

This is strong evidence that silicon-photonics capacity can be contracted and
prepaid at material scale. It is a **supply-chain economics comparator**, not
CPO revenue evidence for Tower or for any of the six companies under review.

It does not identify:

- a CPO product, customer, SKU, shipment, ports or systems;
- CPO versus pluggable/NPO revenue allocation;
- a PIC, engine, laser, package or test content boundary;
- product margin, yield, rework, warranty, capex return or supplier share.

## Research implication

The record reinforces the profit-pool control: even a disclosed, contracted
silicon-photonics capacity figure cannot enter a CPO revenue or valuation model
unless the product mix and economic layer are allocated. It is therefore a
useful benchmark for the level of disclosure needed before translating capacity
commitments into CPO earnings.
