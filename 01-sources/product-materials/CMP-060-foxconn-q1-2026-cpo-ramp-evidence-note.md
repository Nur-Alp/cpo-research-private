# CMP-060 — Foxconn Q1 2026 CPO manufacturing outlook

**Canonical sources:** [Foxconn Q1 2026 results release](https://www.foxconn.com/en-us/press-center/press-releases/latest-news?category=Monthly+Revenues) · [Q1 2026 investor-conference transcript (PDF)](https://image.honhai.com/upload/202605/law_talk/Hon_Hai_1Q26_Results_Transcript_English_20260514_0894_20260526_8671.pdf)  
**Publisher:** Hon Hai Technology Group (Foxconn)  
**Publication date:** 14 May 2026  
**Local files:** [official web record](CMP-060-foxconn-q1-2026-results.html) · [full 28-page transcript](CMP-060-foxconn-q1-2026-investor-transcript.pdf)

## Evidence extracted

- In its Q1 2026 investor conference, Foxconn said that CPO optical-switch mass-production shipments were scheduled to begin in Q3 2026 and that full-year shipments *may* reach the tens-of-thousands of units. The official results release gives the same outlook.
- Foxconn also said CPO and 1.6T high-end-switch products were being developed and prepared for mass production with major cloud and AI-data-center customers, with shipments expected to begin in Q3.
- The company describes capability-building across switch design and assembly, optical modules, optoelectronic integration, cables, connectors, high-speed transmission and power management.

## Correct use

This is a first-party **manufacturer outlook** supporting a broader 2026 manufacturing-ramp hypothesis. It is an independent signal from Foxconn, which NVIDIA names as a Spectrum-X system assembler, but the Foxconn statement does **not** identify NVIDIA, Broadcom, Spectrum-X, TH6-Davisson, a CPO SKU, a customer, an accepted unit count or a supplier allocation. It must not be attached to either NVIDIA or Broadcom commercial-proof numerator.

## Limitations

- The Q3 start and tens-of-thousands figure are forward-looking management expectations, not observed shipment results.
- “Major cloud and AI data center customers” are unnamed; the record does not state a configuration, product maker, acceptance status, port count, repeat shipment or installed base.
- The capabilities list describes potential integration scope, not a contract, bill of materials, qualified share, transfer price, yield, rework, warranty or margin allocation.
- The retained transcript and web release were reviewed for the CPO statements; no claim is made that they establish actual Q3 production or full-year volume.

**Use:** timing and manufacturing-route diligence; refresh against Foxconn Q3 results and a named product/customer record. See `CLM-526`–`CLM-527`.
