# CMP-089 — ASE CPO assembly / warpage control evidence

- **Canonical source:** https://ase.aseglobal.com/press-room/ase-demonstrates-cpo-for-ai-applications/
- **Publisher:** ASE
- **Published:** 2025-04-01
- **Reviewed:** 2026-08-13
- **Source class:** OSAT company demonstration / technical claim

## Bounded extraction

ASE reports a CPO demonstration with multiple optical engines mounted on a
substrate. It identifies assembly-process control of substrate warpage and
coplanarity for fibre-array coupling, including edge and surface coupling, as
part of its CPO assembly flow.

## Permitted use

This is direct OSAT evidence that post-attach package geometry is a material
manufacturing variable. It supports the package/thermal risk map.

## Not permitted

The company does not disclose the product/customer, package or optical-engine
yield, test and qualification population, rework, field reliability, cost or
supplier economics. Its power assertions are not used as measured comparisons.
