# PRI-035 — Lumentum OFC 2026 optical-infrastructure update

- **Primary record:** [local HTML](PRI-035-lumentum-ofc-2026-optical-infrastructure.html) | [canonical Lumentum blog](https://www.lumentum.com/en/blog/enabling-next-phase-ai-optical-infrastructure)
- **Date:** 30 April 2026.

## Evidence

Lumentum states that 1.6T optics and 200G/lane technologies are moving from feasibility into deployment, CPO is advancing toward industrialization, and 400G/lane is the next development target. It describes two live NVIDIA 1.6T 2×DR4 modules: one using its UHP laser as a CW source for an NVIDIA silicon-photonics engine and another using 200G EMLs in an 8×200G architecture.

## Boundary

This is a company technology/marketing update. It identifies Lumentum laser and EML content in demonstrated modules but does not establish CPO production units, engine ownership, qualification, ASP, margin, yield or customer purchase volume.
