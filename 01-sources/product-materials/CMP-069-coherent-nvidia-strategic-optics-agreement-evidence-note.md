# CMP-069 — Coherent NVIDIA strategic optics agreement

- **Publisher:** Coherent
- **Canonical source:** https://www.coherent.com/news/press-releases/nvidia-and-coherent-announce-strategic-partnership
- **Publication:** 2026-03-02
- **Evidence class:** Supplier/customer strategic agreement announcement

Coherent says NVIDIA entered a multiyear strategic agreement covering advanced
optics technologies, manufacturing capacity and R&D. The announcement includes
a multibillion-dollar purchase commitment and future access/capacity rights for
advanced laser and optical-networking products, alongside NVIDIA's $2 billion
investment in Coherent.

**Evidence boundary:** This is strong evidence of strategic commercial
importance and capacity reservation between NVIDIA and Coherent. The public
announcement does not allocate the commitment to Spectrum-X Ethernet CPO,
Quantum-X, pluggable optics or another product family; it does not disclose
units, pricing, qualified share, yield, warranty or CPO-specific margin. Treat
it as an economics-disclosure and supplier-route signal, not CPO revenue.
