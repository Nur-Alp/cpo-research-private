# CMP-044 — POET/LITEON optical-module joint development

- **Primary record:** [retained SEC HTML](CMP-044-poet-liteon-2026-joint-development.html) | [readable PDF snapshot](CMP-044-poet-liteon-2026-joint-development.pdf) | [canonical SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1437424/000117184326001583/exh_991.htm)
- **Date:** 16 March 2026.

## Evidence extracted

POET and LITEON announced joint development of optical modules using POET's Optical Interposer to integrate optical components, drive electronics and coupling structures. POET said prototypes were expected by late 2026 and high-volume production was anticipated for 2027.

## Boundary and limitations

This is a collaboration and roadmap announcement. It does not disclose a customer SKU, purchase order, production units, qualification lot, optical-engine yield, cycle time, ASP, margin or field reliability.

## Research use

Use CMP-044 as corroborating ecosystem and manufacturing-partner evidence, not as a production or revenue datapoint.
