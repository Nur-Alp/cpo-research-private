## Scope decision

Only the optics section is in the active CPO/PIC scope. The SiTime, AMD, ARM, CoreWeave, power-semiconductor, and general trading sections are retained as context but do not create CPO claims.

## Optical evidence and diligence prompts

The optical discussion is useful because it asks for device-specific evidence rather than aggregate capacity claims. The relevant questions are:

1. What are the intrinsic and effective linewidth, RIN, temperature, and qualified lifetime of each high-power laser class?
2. What does “yield” mean for each six-inch InP product bin—photodiode, CW laser power class, 100G/200G EML, or qualified engine output?
3. Does an ELSFP actually remove DSP/retimer and analog-driver/TIA content at the stated product boundary, and what service, fibre-distribution, and warranty costs remain?
4. Are 100G/lane VCSEL NPO demonstrations being incorrectly extrapolated to 200G/lane or 400G/lane reliability?
5. Are GlobalFoundries/Tower hybrid-bonding claims supported by matched S-parameter, thermal, yield, and customer data?

Existing primary anchors include CMP-016/017 (Lumentum UHP/ELSFP), PAP-019/022/025/027, CMP-015 (Coherent OFC demonstrations), PRS-003, and the retained company filings. The roundup does not provide a complete product-level margin, yield, qualification, or customer-volume record.

## Claims deliberately not accepted as facts

The post’s claims of Lumentum superiority, Coherent six-inch “faceplant,” 200G/400G VCSEL failure, ELSFP profit capture, and fab/platform leadership are author opinions or hypotheses. They require primary test reports, qualification records, contracts, and product-level economics.

## Research actions created

- Split six-inch manufacturing evidence into wafer starts, die-bin yield, sellable-device yield, qualified high-power-laser/EML yield, and final-engine yield.
- Add 5,000-hour GR-468 status and failure-mode evidence as a hard laser gate.
- Separate ELSFP component content from complete CPO-engine content in the profit-pool model.
- Keep 100G VCSEL NPO evidence separate from 200G/400G-lane claims.
