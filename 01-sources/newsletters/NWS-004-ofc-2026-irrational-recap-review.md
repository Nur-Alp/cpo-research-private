## Why it is retained

The recap is a map of OFC 2026 topics relevant to the scale-out optical-engine thesis: VCSEL versus silicon photonics, high-power lasers and linewidth, ring-modulator sensitivity, CPO reliability debugging, 400G EML versus SiPho demonstrations, connector/electrical-channel behavior, and LPO/XPO thermal and analog boundaries.

## Evidence that can be used

The review creates four bounded diligence prompts:

1. Dense CPO/NPO demonstrations should be evaluated with all lanes active and with crosstalk, TDECQ/RLM, BER/FEC, temperature, and test-duration boundaries disclosed.
2. Ring-based PIC comparisons should include laser linewidth, thermal detuning/control power, process variation, and effective rather than only intrinsic linewidth.
3. Reliability observations from Broadcom/Meta conference material should be recovered as primary slides or test reports, including failure mechanisms, updated build standards, stress conditions, and population boundaries.
4. A 400G EML-versus-SiPho demonstration is not an apples-to-apples comparison when one path uses 2 km of fibre and the other uses a short loopback; reach and channel boundaries must be matched.

## Claims deliberately not accepted as facts

The recap's statements about 200G VCSEL reliability, Lumentum's unique laser position, NVIDIA or Broadcom unit shipments, vendor share, wafer yield, company valuation, and supplier leadership are author opinions or secondary observations. The recap's reported booth measurements are not entered as product or qualification evidence without the original OFC presentation, test data, or company document.

## Research actions created

- Add all-lane-active, crosstalk, TDECQ/RLM, BER/FEC, and test-duration fields to the optical-engine evidence checklist.
- Request primary OFC slides for the Broadcom/Meta reliability update and the EML/SiPho comparison.
- Add linewidth, RIN, mode-hop, thermal-tuning, and effective-linewidth fields to the laser architecture benchmark.
- Keep Arista XPO/LPO and Ciena electrical-channel observations in the alternative-architecture queue, not in the scale-out PIC leader scorecard.
