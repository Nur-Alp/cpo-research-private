## Decision-relevant observations

1. The post frames 200G electrical SerDes reach and CPO reliability/serviceability as the key reasons CPO may become economically relevant. This aligns with the project’s LPO electrical-boundary work, but the post does not supply a measured 200G system result.
2. It highlights Broadcom’s field-replaceable laser concept as a response to CPO serviceability risk. The claim must be checked against Broadcom’s own product documentation and the OIF external-light-source management boundary; it does not establish complete-engine serviceability.
3. It treats Lumentum, Coherent and Fabrinet as differentiated optical suppliers, while acknowledging that proprietary-edge claims are difficult to verify. This is a useful prompt to demand customer qualification, yield, content and margin evidence rather than accept supplier marketing.
4. It mentions Ayar Labs financing and possible future NVIDIA use. The financing can be checked against Ayar’s official announcement; the deployment rumor is not evidence and is excluded from the company scorecard.
