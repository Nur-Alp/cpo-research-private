## What it establishes

The 400GPL Study Group objectives include optional single-lane 400 Gb/s attachment-unit interfaces for chip-to-module and chip-to-chip applications. They also set study objectives for 400 Gb/s operation over one pair of single-mode fibre to at least 500 m and over twin-axial copper cable to at least 1 m. The non-rate-specific objective includes BER better than or equal to 10^-13 at the MAC/PLS service interface, or the frame-loss equivalent.

The IEEE project page says the group was chartered in March 2026 to develop a PAR and CSD for 400 Gb/s-per-lane electrical interconnects and SMF optical interconnects up to 500 m. The page lists the PAR/CSD as approved by IEEE 802 LMSC on 17 July 2026 and the objectives as approved by the Working Group on 16 July 2026.

## Boundary and limitation

These are project objectives, not a ratified Ethernet standard, compliance test, electrical insertion-loss budget, FEC allocation, or product qualification. They establish that an interoperable 400G/lane C2M/C2C boundary is being pursued and that the objective is intentionally compatible with multiple physical implementations. They do not select LPO, NPO or CPO, and they do not prove a measured 400G/lane link or commercial deployment.
