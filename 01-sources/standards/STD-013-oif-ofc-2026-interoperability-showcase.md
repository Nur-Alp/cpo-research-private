# STD-013 — OIF OFC 2026 interoperability showcase

Canonical publisher page: <https://www.oiforum.com/oif-validates-critical-interoperability-live-at-ofc-2026-through-multi-vendor-demonstrations-and-expert-panels/>

Reviewed 2026-08-07. OIF reported that 40 member companies participated in the OFC 2026 interoperability showcase. The announced technology areas included CEI-448G and CEI-224G, co-packaging, EEI, CMIS and ELSFP; OIF described live demonstrations across retimed, half-retimed (RTLR) and unretimed (linear) pluggable optics and silicon, plus a live CMIS-managed ELSFP demonstration. OIF also described a live 448G demonstration and an expanded 224G showcase across link types.

Evidence boundary: this is an OIF event/showcase report, not a retained measurement dataset or production qualification record. It confirms ecosystem activity and multi-vendor demonstration scope, but not lane-level BER, power, yield, field reliability, customer deployment, ASP or margin.
