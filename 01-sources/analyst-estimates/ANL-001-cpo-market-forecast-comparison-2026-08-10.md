## Modelling rule

Do **not** average these forecasts. Until the definition of each addressable market is reconciled, the only allowable use is as a wide external scenario envelope. The private CPO revenue bridge must continue to begin with customer systems, adoption, engines per system, supplier content and qualified share—not a top-down market-report value.

## Current interpretation

- **Fact:** The pages above publicly state their respective forecasts.
- **Inference:** The dispersion itself is evidence that a generic “CPO market size” is not decision-useful without a defined product boundary.
- **Open question:** Which portions, if any, include switch systems, modules, optical engines, PICs, external lasers, packaging and services?
