# PAP-045 — Li et al., 1.6 Tbps FOWLP-Based Silicon Photonic Engine

## Citation

Xin Li et al., “1.6 Tbps FOWLP-Based Silicon Photonic Engine for Co-Packaged Optics,” *Journal of Lightwave Technology* 43, pp. 1979–1986 (2025). DOI: [10.1109/JLT.2024.3493855](https://doi.org/10.1109/JLT.2024.3493855). Canonical publisher record: [Optica abstract page](https://opg.optica.org/jlt/abstract.cfm?uri=jlt-43-4-1979).

## What was reviewed

The complete eight-page journal PDF was retrieved from Downloads and reviewed on 2026-08-10.

## Bounded evidence

- The paper demonstrates a 1.6-Tb/s-class silicon-photonic engine using eight 224-Gb/s/lambda channels and electronic-photonic FOWLP integration without wire bonds.
- It reports 112-Gbaud NRZ and 112-Gbaud PAM4 (224 Gb/s) transmitter demonstrations, including a direct-drive configuration and wafer-level electrical/optical testing.
- The fabrication flow embeds known-good PICs in a reconstituted molded wafer, protects optical edge couplers during molding and routes RF signals through the organic substrate.
- Reported transmitter measurements include PAM4 TDECQ of about 2.08 dB with 31-tap equalization and about 2.32 dB in the direct-drive configuration; the paper also reports 112-Gb/s NRZ eyes and receiver measurements.
- “Low-cost” and “volume-manufacturable” are the authors’ framing of the platform, not measured production economics.

## Evidence boundary

This is a peer-reviewed engine/packaging comparator, not evidence of a customer-qualified CPO system. The paper does not provide production-lot yield, process capability, test/rework economics, thermal/reliability qualification, customer SKU, ASP, margin or supplier allocation. Claims are recorded as `CLM-438`–`CLM-440` and `CLM-487`–`CLM-490`.

## Research use

Use PAP-045 to compare the FOWLP package route against PAP-029’s conference demonstration and PAP-044’s TGV-interposer engine. Do not use it to upgrade the commercial-proof probability, adoption-share denominator or profit-pool ranking until the full article and production evidence are available.
