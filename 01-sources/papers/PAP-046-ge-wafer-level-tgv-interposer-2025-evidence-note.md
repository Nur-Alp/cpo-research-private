# PAP-046 — Ge et al., wafer-level TGV interposer for 2.5D CPO

## Citation

Chang Ge et al., “High-speed wafer-level TGV interposer for 2.5D CPO,” *Optics Communications* 579 (2025), article 131517. DOI: [10.1016/j.optcom.2025.131517](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.optcom.2025.131517). Publisher record: [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0030401825000458). A preprint record is available at [SSRN 5016876](https://ssrn.com/abstract=5016876).

## What was reviewed

The complete seven-page article PDF was reviewed page-by-page on 2026-08-10 and is retained locally. The publisher abstract and SSRN bibliographic record were used for cross-checking.

## Bounded evidence

- The abstract reports an 8-inch wafer-level TGV interposer fabricated with Laser-Induced Deep Etching (LIDE) and metallization.
- It reports 110 GHz bandwidth and a 128-Gbaud OOK signal interconnection, using simulation and experimental verification.
- It describes flip-chip integration of driver and electro-absorption-modulated laser (EML) chips onto the interposer.
- Optoelectronic joint testing reports maximum EML optical output of −0.07 dBm.
- The measured TGV/RDL transmission line reaches 110 GHz bandwidth and measured 90, 100 and 128-Gb/s OOK eye diagrams are shown (pp. 3–4).
- The authors package an EML chip on the TGV interposer by flip-chip bonding, but the initial validation uses probes and a fibre lens rather than a completed driver-plus-EML system (pp. 4–6).
- The post-package EML maximum optical output is −0.07 dBm versus 4.9 dBm before packaging; the paper attributes the loss to fibre-lens coupling and possible flip-chip pressure/damage (p. 6).

## Evidence boundary

This is a technical interposer/active-chip demonstration, not a qualified complete CPO engine. The paper does not provide BER/TDECQ/FEC, fibre reach, thermal/reliability qualification, lot yield, test/rework, customer SKU, ASP, margin or production volume. It explicitly exposes a packaging/coupling loss boundary in the EML test. Claims are recorded as `CLM-445`–`CLM-447` and `CLM-476`–`CLM-478`.

## Research use

Use PAP-046 to extend the TGV/glass comparator alongside PAP-044 and WHT-002. It strengthens the technical packaging route but does not upgrade commercial-proof probabilities or the optical-engine profit-pool ranking.
