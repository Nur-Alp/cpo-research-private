## Full-paper evidence

- The paper demonstrates a **4 × 100-Gbps/λ PAM4 optical engine** using a 750-µm-thick TGV interposer and localized 808-nm laser soldering for replacement.
- It reports measured electrical-to-optical **S21 −3 dB bandwidth of 51.8 GHz**, **TDECQ of 1.6 dB or less on all lanes**, and real-time 4 × 106-Gbps operation at the KP4-FEC threshold with receiver sensitivity better than **−8.3 dBm**.
- The engine is tested at 25°C and 85°C and after replacement. The re-soldered engine shows comparable average launch power, extinction ratio and TDECQ, supporting a repairability demonstration.
- The paper reports 10-km transmission BER measurements and uses a CuW package, PCB and PAM4 DSP evaluation board; it remains an on-board/pluggable optical-engine demonstration rather than a CPO switch package.

## Boundary and limitations

The full ten-page paper is now retained and reviewed. It still does not disclose complete engine power, lot-level yield, environmental qualification, automated replacement cycle time, cost, customer deployment, ASP or margin. The 400-Gbps result is an optical-engine demonstration for on-board/pluggable applications; it is not evidence of a 400G/lane CPO production system. Claims are recorded as `CLM-504`–`CLM-508`.
