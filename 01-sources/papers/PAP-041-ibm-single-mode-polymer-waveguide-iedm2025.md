# PAP-041 — Co-packaged optics module with single-mode polymer waveguide

- **Authors:** Akihiro Horibe, Yoichi Taira, Sayuri Kohara, Chinami Marushima, Hiroyuki Mori, Hidetoshi Numata, Jean Benoit Heroux, Adrian Paz Ramos, Hsiang Han Hsu, Neng Liu, Mark Schultz and John Knickerbocker
- **Organisation:** IBM Research
- **Publication:** IEDM 2025, 6 December 2025
- **Canonical research record:** https://research.ibm.com/publications/co-packaged-optics-module-with-single-mode-polymer-waveguide
- **Evidence class:** Official research abstract / bibliographic record

IBM reports CPO modules using single-mode polymer-waveguide fibre interfaces. The abstract states that two substrate configurations were evaluated, including an optics-last assembly variant, and that mechanical and thermomechanical reliability testing was performed. It reports stable adhesion, insertion loss in the <1.2–2.0 dB range and resilience under thermal stress, and describes a three-port CPO module with a substrate cut-out.

**Evidence boundary:** This is a useful full-module process and reliability record, but the public page does not disclose sample counts, channel-by-channel loss distributions, stress duration, pass/fail statistics, production yield, customer qualification, field returns, ASP or margin. The insertion-loss range must not be treated as a complete 200G/lane engine result.
