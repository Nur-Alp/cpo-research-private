# PAP-036 — Intel PIPES fiber-based CPO assembly proof of concept

- **Authors:** Intel and collaborating university/industrial teams
- **Canonical record:** https://doi.org/10.1109/ECTC51687.2025.00061
- **Publication:** IEEE ECTC 2025
- **Evidence class:** Conference-paper bibliographic/abstract record; full text not retained

The paper record describes a DARPA PIPES/CHIPS prototype multi-chip package using an open-cavity bridge and Intel EMIB. The compute tile is connected to data-converter dies and three EIC-PIC stacks, with three fibre-array units representing 56 fibre couplers at 127 µm pitch. The record identifies collaboration across Cornell (EIC), Columbia/AIM Photonics (PIC), SUNY (PIC manufacturing) and Tyndall (fibre attach), and reports a proof-of-concept fiber-based butt-coupled assembly.

**Evidence boundary:** This establishes a heterogeneous assembly architecture and supplier-role map, not a production package. The retained record provides no complete-link loss, sample yield, reflow/thermal qualification, cycle time, field reliability, customer SKU, ASP or margin.
