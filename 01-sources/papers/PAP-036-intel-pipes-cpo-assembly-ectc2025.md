# PAP-036 — Intel PIPES fiber-based CPO assembly proof of concept

- **Authors:** Intel and collaborating university/industrial teams
- **Canonical record:** https://doi.org/10.1109/ECTC51687.2025.00061
- **Publication:** IEEE ECTC 2025
- **Evidence class:** Full seven-page conference paper retained and reviewed

The paper describes a DARPA PIPES/CHIPS prototype multi-chip package using an open-cavity bridge and Intel EMIB. The compute tile is connected to data-converter dies and three EIC-PIC stacks, with three fibre-array units representing 56 fibre couplers at 127 µm pitch. The paper also documents die thinning/carrier handling, substrate thermal exposure and assembly-yield loss: approximately 50% substrate loss after thermal pre-screening and approximately 90% cumulative loss after later thermal-compression/attach exposures in the reported prototype flow.

**Evidence boundary:** This establishes a heterogeneous assembly architecture and exposes a severe prototype substrate/thermal-yield issue; it is not a production package. The retained record provides no complete-link loss, accepted-unit denominator for the whole package, production Cpk, cycle time, field reliability, customer SKU, ASP or margin. Claims are recorded as `CLM-491`–`CLM-494`.
