# PAP-053 evidence note — Uncooled O-band InP MZ Modulator PIC

**Citation:** Yoshihiro Ogiso et al., “Uncooled O-band InP MZ Modulator PIC for 3.2 Tb/s (400 Gb/s/lane) Pluggable Transceiver,” OFC Postdeadline Papers 2025, Th4D.1, DOI 10.1364/OFC.2025.Th4D.1. [Optica abstract](https://opg.optica.org/abstract.cfm?uri=OFC-2025-Th4D.1)

## Full-paper evidence

- The three-page OFC postdeadline paper reports a 100-GHz-class-bandwidth, TEC-less O-band eight-channel InP Mach–Zehnder modulator PIC.
- The PIC uses 625-µm channel pitch, 65-Ω differential termination, thermo-optic phase adjusters and spot-size converters; the reported per-MZM insertion loss is about 12 dB at the stated operating point.
- The authors report 400-Gb/s/lane operation and net 3.2-Tb/s transmission over 500 m from 20–80°C. BER is reported as broadly temperature-insensitive across the eight channels; receiver sensitivity is around +8 dBm with a TIA-less photodiode in the stated test.
- Far-end crosstalk between adjacent channels is reported below −30 dB to 90 GHz.

## Why it matters

This is a useful 400G/lane counterweight to the 180-GBaud PAP-051 engine record: it suggests that an uncooled InP MZ PIC can reach the lane-rate and temperature boundary without a TEC in a pluggable test. It is relevant to the PIC/modulator choice and to the CPO-versus-pluggable deferral question.

## Evidence boundary

The full paper is a short component/transmission demonstration. It does not disclose a complete driver/PIC co-package, module/chassis power, fibre attach, process yield, reliability qualification, field service, customer adoption, ASP or margin. Treat it as a strong 400G/lane pluggable counterweight, not proof of a production-qualified CPO optical engine. Claims are recorded as `CLM-483`–`CLM-486`.
