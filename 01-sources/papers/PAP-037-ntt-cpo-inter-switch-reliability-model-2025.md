# PAP-037 — NTT reliability model for inter-switch links using CPO

- **Authors:** Tsuyoshi Ito, Yusuke Muranaka, Yuki Arikawa and Kazunori Seno
- **Affiliation:** NTT
- **Canonical record:** https://ken.ieice.org/ken/paper/202511113cOl/eng/
- **Publication:** IEICE Technical Report, PN2025-21, 2025-11-03
- **Evidence class:** Technical-report abstract and bibliographic record; full PDF not retained

The report proposes a reliability model for inter-switch links that explicitly reflects the internal structure of CPO rather than treating the link as a conventional undifferentiated path. It compares the proposed simplified method with a conventional reliability model and reports an evaluation of the difference.

**Evidence boundary:** The record establishes a modeling approach and reinforces that CPO reliability must be decomposed by internal component and link structure. It does not provide a field-return population, measured failure rate, MTBF, engine replacement procedure, warranty allocation, production yield or economic result.
