# PAP-049 — Gao et al., heterogeneous integration review

## Citation

Han Gao, Wanyi Yan, Dan Zhang and Daquan Yu, “Heterogeneous Integration Technology Drives the Evolution of Co-Packaged Optics,” *Micromachines* 16(9), 1037 (10 September 2025), DOI [10.3390/mi16091037](https://doi.org/10.3390/mi16091037). [Open-access publisher record](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-666X/16/9/1037) · [PMC full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12471338/) · [Local PDF](PAP-049-zhang-heterogeneous-integration-cpo-review-2025.pdf).

## What was reviewed

The complete 53-page open-access PDF was retrieved and reviewed on 2026-08-10. This is a review article synthesizing prior work, not a new production dataset.

## Bounded evidence

- The review separates FOWLP, TSV and TGV routes and compares signal integrity, thermal management, optical coupling, manufacturing complexity, reliability and cost (pp. 5–7, 19–20).
- It reports the reviewed 51.2-Tb/s FOWLP prototype boundary, including 0.9 pJ/bit and 64 activated channels, while identifying the result as a prototype rather than production output (p. 8).
- It summarizes a TGV 400-Gbps optical-engine record with 51.8-GHz E/O bandwidth, TDECQ below 1.6 dB and maintainability/replacement evidence (p. 16); these are cited literature results, not a new experiment in this review.
- It identifies thermal density, yield, reliability, maintainability and manufacturing scale as coupled CPO constraints (pp. 10–20).

## Evidence boundary

PAP-049 is a full-text review and source map. It does not establish a new customer-qualified engine, production yield, field failure distribution, ASP, margin or supplier profit. Claims are recorded as `CLM-454`–`CLM-457`.

## Research use

Use it to structure the FOWLP-versus-TSV-versus-TGV packaging comparison and to locate primary papers for follow-up. Keep the review’s market or architecture synthesis separate from measured primary evidence and from investment forecasts.
