# PAP-051 — Tran et al., 180-GBaud driver–modulator engine

## Citation

Son Tran et al., “180 GBaud PAM4 Driver-Modulator Engine for IM/DD Transmissions in the O-Band,” OFC 2026, paper W3E.6, DOI [10.1364/OFC.2026.W3E.6](https://doi.org/10.1364/OFC.2026.W3E.6). [Optica abstract record](https://opg.optica.org/abstract.cfm?uri=OFC-2026-W3E.6).

## What was reviewed

The complete three-page OFC PDF was retrieved through university access, visually checked page by page, and retained locally on 2026-08-13.

## Bounded evidence

- The authors report a co-packaged 76-GHz InP Mach–Zehnder modulator and a 224-GBaud-class linear differential EML driver.
- The engine achieved 180-GBaud PAM4 back-to-back O-band operation with BER below the 20%-overhead hard-decision FEC threshold under 0.4 Vppd driver excitation.
- The measurement was conducted at 40°C under thermoelectric-cooler control. Reported 525 mW / 1.45 pJ per bit includes only driver, MZM and RF-signal power; DSP, external laser and TEC power are explicitly excluded.
- The authors are affiliated with Fraunhofer HHI/TU Berlin and MACOM, making this a relevant component/engine boundary for the 400G/lane workstream.

## Evidence boundary

PAP-051 is a measured driver–modulator subassembly record, not a completed optical-engine or system record. It remains back-to-back with external lab instrumentation, a TEC, an external laser and a 2,048-tap receiver DSP. It does not disclose fibre attach, link reach, complete engine/system power, yield, qualification, customer deployment, ASP or margin. Claims are recorded as `CLM-461`–`CLM-463` and `CLM-582`–`CLM-584`.

## Research use

Use it as a measured 400G-class driver/modulator checkpoint. Do not treat 180-GBaud back-to-back operation as a qualified 400G/lane production link.
