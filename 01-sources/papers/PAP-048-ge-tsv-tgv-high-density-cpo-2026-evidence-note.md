# PAP-048 — Ge et al., high-density TSV/TGV CPO interposers

## Citation

Chang Ge, Jiangbing Du, Yihan Liu, Yixiao Zhang and Zuyuan He, “High-density co-packaged optics based on TSV and TGV interposers for advanced optical interconnection,” *Advanced Photonics Nexus* 5(3), 036019 (25 May 2026), DOI [10.1117/1.APN.5.3.036019](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.APN.5.3.036019). [Publisher record](https://www.spiedigitallibrary.org/journals/advanced-photonics-nexus/volume-5/issue-03/036019/High-density-co-packaged-optics-based-on-TSV-and-TGV-interposers/10.1117/1.APN.5.3.036019.full).

## What was reviewed

The publisher abstract and bibliographic record were reviewed on 2026-08-09. The full article was not retrieved in the current environment; this is an abstract-level source record.

## Bounded evidence

- The abstract reports fabricated TSV and TGV interposers with 3-dB bandwidths exceeding 67 GHz and 110 GHz, respectively, supporting 128-Gbaud transmission.
- It describes proposed 2.5D/3D CPO optical-engine architectures and 112-GBaud operation as a design boundary.
- The record positions TSV/TGV integration as a route toward higher density and lower power, but does not provide a complete production engine, lot yield, qualification or customer evidence.

## Evidence boundary

PAP-048 is useful for the TSV/TGV packaging and 400G-class electrical-interface comparator. It does not establish complete-engine BER/TDECQ, power, thermal lifetime, final yield, rework, ASP, margin or deployment volume. Claims are recorded as `CLM-451`–`CLM-453`.

## Research use

Use this paper to update the packaging benchmark and to separate measured interposer bandwidth from the much harder final-engine manufacturing and service gates. Keep it distinct from `PAP-046`, which is an earlier abstract-level TGV interposer record with a different journal and evidence boundary.
