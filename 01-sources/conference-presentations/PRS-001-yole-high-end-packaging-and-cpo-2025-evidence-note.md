# PRS-001 — Yole high-end packaging and CPO deck

## Review record

The retained PDF was inspected on 2026-08-09. It is a 28-page Yole Group presentation by Vishal Saroha for the SEMI 3D & Systems Summit 2025. Text extraction and page-level review confirmed the CPO sections on pages 13–19; the file is not being treated as a primary research paper.

## Relevant content

- Page 13 separates EOI, OBO, NPO and CPO and distinguishes scale-out from scale-up CPO.
- Page 14 presents Yole’s estimated time-to-market paths for networking CPO, HPC/AI-ML CPO, disaggregated memory and resource pooling.
- Page 15 presents a datacom CPO revenue estimate of approximately $46M in 2024 and $5.4B in 2030, with a stated 121% CAGR, split between scale-up and scale-out.
- Page 17 maps Intel, ASE, NVIDIA and TSMC packaging approaches; page 18 maps the silicon-photonics value chain.
- Page 19 lists testing/debugging, thermal management, power, integration complexity, interoperability, cost, reliability/maintainability, scalability and supply-chain maturity as challenges.

## Evidence boundary

The market sizes and timing chart are Yole estimates. The deck does not provide a reproducible unit/ASP model, customer shipment denominator, confidence interval, supplier revenue allocation or independent validation. Use it for taxonomy, market framing and diligence-question generation—not as evidence of CPO production, adoption share or profit-pool size. Claims are recorded as `CLM-441`–`CLM-444`.
