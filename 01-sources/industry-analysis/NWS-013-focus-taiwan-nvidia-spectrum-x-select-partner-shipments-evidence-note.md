# NWS-013 — Focus Taiwan: NVIDIA Spectrum-X CPO select-partner shipments

**Canonical source:** <https://focustaiwan.tw/business/202606030020>  
**Publisher:** Focus Taiwan / Central News Agency (CNA)  
**Publication date:** 2026-06-03  
**Local file:** [HTML snapshot](NWS-013-focus-taiwan-nvidia-spectrum-x-select-partner-shipments.html)

## Evidence extracted

Focus Taiwan reports from NVIDIA GTC Taipei that Gilad Shainer, NVIDIA’s senior vice president of networking, said NVIDIA had begun shipping its new Spectrum-X CPO switch to select partners. The report describes it as developed with TSMC and identifies up to 400Tb/s switching capacity.

## Correct use

This is a dated, independently reported attribution of NVIDIA executive shipment language. It corroborates the select-partner shipment status that later appears in TrendForce’s `NWS-012`; it does **not** identify the shipping SKU as `SN6800` or `SN6810`, name any recipient, distinguish evaluation from accepted commercial deployment, give units/ports, or establish repeat delivery.

It can therefore strengthen the **shipment-status** row of the NVIDIA dossier, but cannot clear the customer, scale, repeatability, supplier-content or profit-capture gates.

## Limitations

- News-agency report of an NVIDIA executive statement, not a customer procurement/acceptance record.
- “Select partners” is unnamed and unquantified.
- The report supplies no product serial/configuration, acceptance date, shipment count, installed-base, field-service, yield, ASP or margin data.
- It must not be used to link CoreWeave, Lambda, OCI or any other named NVIDIA ecosystem partner to the CPO shipments.

**Use:** independent secondary corroboration of limited/select-partner shipment status only. See `CLM-521`.
