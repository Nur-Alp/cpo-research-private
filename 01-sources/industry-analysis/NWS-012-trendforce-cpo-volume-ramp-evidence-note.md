# NWS-012 — TrendForce CPO volume-ramp and bottleneck report

**Canonical source:** <https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20260727-13151.html>  
**Publisher:** TrendForce  
**Publication date:** 2026-07-27  
**Local files:** [HTML snapshot](NWS-012-trendforce-cpo-volume-ramp.html) · [readable PDF snapshot](NWS-012-trendforce-cpo-volume-ramp.pdf)

## Evidence extracted

- TrendForce says NVIDIA began shipping Spectrum-X CPO switches to select partners and Broadcom continued limited shipments of its 51.2T Bailly switch.
- It identifies optical-engine yield, silicon-photonics wafer/packaging capacity and advanced packaging as supply constraints for volume expansion.
- It describes NVIDIA's Spectrum-X CPO as jointly developed with TSMC and using COUPE, with capacity expansion expected in the second half of 2026.
- It says the CPO switch market's volume ramp is expected in 2027–2028 and that vendors controlling silicon-photonics design, optical-engine manufacturing and advanced packaging are best positioned.
- It repeats a 0.5% 2026 CPO share estimate and a possible 35% 2030 penetration estimate in related TrendForce research; those figures are not used as this project's base case.

## Correct use

This is stronger than the XenoSpectrum secondary summary because it is the direct TrendForce press-center record, but it is still an analyst/research-house publication rather than a customer shipment filing. It supports the timing and bottleneck hypotheses, not a precise customer numerator, final yield or profit forecast.

## Limitations

- No named customer SKU, units, repeat order, qualification lot, final-engine yield, ASP, margin or field data.
- “Select partners” and “limited shipments” remain unquantified.
- Market penetration estimates may use an optical-module denominator that differs from this project's system-based adoption definition.

**Use:** secondary market/timing triangulation and supply-chain diligence; not a company-specific forecast. See `CLM-416`–`CLM-420`.
