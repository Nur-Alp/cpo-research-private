# Marvell / Celestial AI: Accelerator Optical-I/O Dossier

**Status:** Evidence-matched initial dossier; not an investment recommendation
**Deployment domain:** Accelerator scale-up, including within-/across-rack XPU interconnect
**Last updated:** 2026-08-07

## Bottom line

Marvell has the clearest current public-company route to accelerator optical I/O because it acquired Celestial AI and its Photonic Fabric platform. The acquisition establishes strategic ownership and makes future revenue milestones observable. It does **not** establish a qualified production leader, an achieved revenue run rate, or a superior return on the acquisition.[CLM-093][CLM-095][CLM-096]

This is a separate thesis from switch-side CPO. The relevant questions are scale-up protocol/topology, XPU/package integration, thermal and memory architecture, optical-chiplet yield, customer qualification, and replacement/service model—not the merchant Ethernet-switch engine alone.

| Dimension | Evidence | Boundary |

| Current financial evidence | Marvell reported $2.418B Q1 FY27 revenue and includes Celestial from acquisition date. [CLM-096] | No Photonic Fabric/Celestial revenue or gross margin is disclosed. |

## What Marvell may capture

If qualified, Marvell could control high-value scale-up connectivity content that includes optical chiplets plus complementary custom XPU, switching, DSP and protocol assets. That is strategically different from selling an external laser or an Ethernet optical engine. The public record is insufficient to assign which electrical, optical, packaging, test, laser and service content remains inside Marvell versus external suppliers.

## Principal risks

1. **Architecture risk:** Copper, AECs, NPO, CPO, alternative optical chiplets or different scale-up protocols may meet the customer requirement without Photonic Fabric.
2. **Packaging and thermal risk:** The claimed vertically co-packaged optical path is only as investable as its final package yield, laser/photonic reliability, test flow and XPU thermal behavior.
3. **Customer/concentration risk:** No customer, design win, volume, price, qualification or cancellation protection is publicly disclosed.
4. **Economic risk:** No product ASP, gross margin, capex, support cost or external component content has been disclosed.
5. **Acquisition/earnout risk:** The expected revenue path is a management forecast. Revenue-based contingent consideration can dilute shareholders and its fair-value remeasurement can affect reported results.[CLM-093][CLM-095]

## Required evidence next

1. Independent product/packaging test or a customer-qualified system with a defined protocol and topology.
2. Named production customer and XPU/switch partner, units, deployment date and repeat order.
3. Measured energy/bit, latency, thermal behavior and reliability at the complete-system boundary versus copper and other optical alternatives.
4. Final optical-chiplet/package yield, test, laser/supply-chain, serviceability and capex data.
5. Product-level revenue, gross margin, customer concentration and the actual progress against FY28/FY29 targets.
