**Status:** Private evidence control; relative stances only, no target prices or numeric CPO sensitivities  
**As of:** 13 August 2026  
**Purpose:** Apply one proof chain to NVIDIA, Broadcom, Coherent, Lumentum,
Marvell and TSMC:

```text
product → customer → supplier role → qualified output → economics
→ catalyst → falsification
```

## Readout

Every company has a standardised proof dossier in this register and a detailed
card in [core-company variant cards](variant-cards/core-company-variant-cards.md).
None clears the revenue, EPS, valuation or profit-pool bridge. The only
permitted current output is a relative strategic stance plus the record that
would upgrade or falsify it.

## Update rule

Update a row only when a source changes one of the seven cells above. Record
