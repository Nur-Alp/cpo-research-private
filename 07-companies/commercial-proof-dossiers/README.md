# Commercial-proof dossiers

**Status:** Private working documents; not cleared for publication  
**As of:** 2026-08-14

These dossiers answer the narrow timing question: whether disclosed switch-side CPO has crossed from vendor/product language into customer-confirmed, repeat commercial deployment. They do not rank equities or infer supplier revenue.

For the concise decision view, see the [one-page commercial-proof decision memo](commercial-proof-decision-memo.md). The two company dossiers below retain the detailed evidence and source boundaries.

The current closeout, including the SKU-level NVIDIA and Broadcom verdicts, is [commercial-proof closeout — 14 August](commercial-proof-closeout-2026-08-14.md).

## Clearance rule

A company clears the commercial-proof gate only when one source bundle identifies all of the following at the same product boundary:

1. Exact CPO SKU/configuration and architecture;
2. Customer acceptance or qualification date;
3. Units, ports or systems over a defined period; and
4. Repeat shipment, expansion or renewal.

The economic gate is separate: engine/PIC/laser/package/test content, supplier share, ASP, qualified yield, warranty and margin must be attributable before any CPO profit-pool conclusion.

## Current conclusion

Neither NVIDIA Spectrum-X Ethernet Photonics nor Broadcom TH6-Davisson clears the switch-side commercial-proof gate. NVIDIA has the stronger disclosed integrated production and manufacturing route; Broadcom has the clearer merchant-switch CPO product definition. Both lack a reconciled named-customer CPO SKU, accepted-unit denominator and repeat-shipment record.

## Latest verification result

The 13 August exact-SKU refresh reviewed current first-party NVIDIA product/documentation pages, Broadcom's `BCM78919` catalogue and the named OEM/operator routes in the retrieval log. It found no source bundle that simultaneously identifies:

1. the exact CPO SKU and customer/operator;
2. an acceptance or qualification date;
3. a dated unit, port or system denominator; and
4. a repeat shipment, expansion or renewal.

This is a documented negative retrieval outcome, not a claim that no private deployment exists. Product availability, “now shipping,” “Contact Sales,” “Limited Release,” sampling, partner quotations and a different CPO domain (for example Quantum-X InfiniBand) remain inadmissible as substitutes for the same-SKU customer numerator. See the [SKU-bound customer search audit](../../09-primary-research/sku-customer-search-audit-2026-08-11.md).

The economic gate remains separately blocked for both companies: no exact-SKU record provides attributable PIC/engine, EIC, laser, fibre attach, package, connector or test share together with ASP, qualified yield/rework, warranty and margin.

Use the [exact-SKU evidence-packet template](../../09-primary-research/exact-sku-evidence-packet-template.md)
for every new customer/OEM/integrator result. It forces product boundary,
acceptance, scale, repeatability and supplier-economics fields to be captured
separately, including a documented negative disposition when the result is a
demo, platform signal or ambiguous CPO claim.

The operational search sequence and stop rule are in the [commercial-proof
