# Six-company commercial-proof queue

**Status:** Private diligence control; no public investment conclusion
**As of:** 2026-08-12
**Purpose:** Convert the six-company cards into product-matched evidence requests. This is a queue, not a ranking.

## Readiness rule

A company can enter a numeric CPO sensitivity only when the same product
boundary has: (1) an exact SKU or defined process product, (2) a named
customer/operator or contractual production record, (3) an accepted unit,
port, system or qualified-output denominator, (4) repeatability evidence, and
(5) attributable content, price, share, yield, warranty and margin data.
Strategic partnerships, capacity reservations, architecture demonstrations and
consolidated financial margins do not satisfy these gates.

## Evidence handling by request type

| Request type | Acceptable record | Not sufficient |
|---|---|---|
| Customer deployment | Customer/OEM document naming exact SKU and deployment/acceptance date | Vendor adopter list, platform benchmark or “available/contact sales” label |
| Scale | Accepted ports, systems, units, qualified lots or explicit production denominator | Total platform bandwidth, rack size without switch count, market forecast |
| Repeatability | Second dated shipment, expansion, renewal or repeated qualified lot | “Production,” “shipping,” “ramp,” sampling or one demonstration |
| Supplier content | Product BOM, contract, named qualification and layer boundary | Partnership, ecosystem list, capacity reservation or broad product family |
| Economics | Realised price/share, product margin, yield/rework, warranty and capex tied to the same boundary | Consolidated margin, purchase commitment, SAM, capacity or analyst estimate |

## Current interpretation

The queue supports differentiated stances but not a proven public-equity
leader. NVIDIA has the strongest integrated product/timing signal; Broadcom
has the clearest merchant-switch definition; Lumentum has the strongest
near-term component-order signal; Coherent has the broadest component/capacity
route; TSMC has process-control optionality; Marvell remains an accelerator
optical-I/O watch case. None has cleared the complete commercial-economic chain.

The queue should be refreshed only when a record changes a gate. Do not add
generic CPO articles merely to increase citation count.
