# Company variant cards

Each card converts the restricted consensus baseline and CPO scenario into a decision-ready, falsifiable view. The cards must not become target-price reports. Until analyst reports are ingested, all consensus and EPS/valuation fields remain `Pending`.

## Required card fields

1. Reconciled consensus baseline and as-of date.
2. Observed public CPO facts.
3. CPO contribution implicitly or explicitly expected by the market.
4. Nur Alpys variant view, confidence and rationale.
5. Bear/base/bull incremental EPS and valuation sensitivity.
6. Positive catalyst, downside risk and falsification trigger.
7. Private estimate IDs, public evidence IDs and public-use status.

The [six-company card completion audit](six-company-card-completion-audit-2026-08-12.md)
records the current state of every field. “Not eligible” is intentional when a
product-matched commercial or economic gate is open; it must not be replaced by
an invented estimate.
