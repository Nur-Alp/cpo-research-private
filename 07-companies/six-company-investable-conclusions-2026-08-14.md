# Six CPO company conclusions

**Status:** Private, evidence-gated relative views; not investment advice, target prices or CPO revenue forecasts  
**As of:** 2026-08-14

## Decision rule

An “investable” conclusion here means a decision-ready relative stance with explicit evidence boundaries—not a claim that CPO is material to near-term EPS. Where product-linked customer scale, content attribution and economics are not public, the correct output is watch/neutral.

## Cross-company conclusion

No company clears the minimum CPO profit-pool bundle: same-boundary customer units and repeatability, supplier content/share, final-engine yield/rework, warranty/service burden, ASP and product margin. The strongest current relative positions are **NVIDIA for integrated platform route**, **Broadcom for merchant-switch product definition**, **Lumentum for component-order conversion signal**, and **TSMC for integration-process control**. Those are not interchangeable forms of value capture.

The current investable conclusion is therefore: **maintain company-specific watchlists and relative strategic stances; do not translate CPO into a standalone earnings, EPS, valuation or profit-pool call.**
