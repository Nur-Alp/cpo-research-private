# Fabrinet manufacturing-route dossier

## Role in the CPO profit-pool question

Fabrinet is a candidate manufacturing and advanced-packaging control point, not currently a proven CPO product owner. Its FY2025 filing describes an end-to-end outsourced optical manufacturing flow, NPI-to-volume transfer, reliability qualification and a Thailand production base. That makes it relevant to the question of who captures manufacturing yield, test, rework and scale economics.

## What the filing establishes

| Question | Evidence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Can it support the manufacturing flow? | Process design, supply chain, advanced optical packaging, integration, final assembly and testing are all described [`CLM-265`](../01-sources/claim-ledger.csv). | Capability statement; no CPO SKU or measured engine yield. |
| Can it move from prototype to volume? | NPI centres support DFM and transfer qualified programmes to Thailand; qualification can take three to six months or longer [`CLM-266`](../01-sources/claim-ledger.csv). | General company process; not proof of a CPO qualification. |
| Is the economics baseline attractive? | FY2025 optical communications revenue was $2.619B; gross margin was 12.1%; capex was $130.7M [`CLM-267`](../01-sources/claim-ledger.csv). | Consolidated/market-level denominators; no CPO content or margin. |
| What limits pricing power? | Two customers represented 45.8% of revenue, and most commitments are short-term/project-based [`CLM-268`](../01-sources/claim-ledger.csv). | Customer names and CPO exposure are not disclosed. |
| Does the current business expose the relevant end markets? | Q3 FY2026 DCI revenue was $196.9M and nine-month DCI was $477.2M; HPC revenue was $106.7M in the quarter and $207.7M year to date [`CLM-269`](../01-sources/claim-ledger.csv). | DCI/HPC are broad categories, not CPO proof. |
| Where can manufacturing economics fail? | Fixed quarterly pricing assumes yields and quality; the filing explicitly identifies lower yields, defects, correction, warranty and penalty costs as risks [`CLM-271`](../01-sources/claim-ledger.csv). | No CPO contract or quantified loss waterfall. |

## Investment implication

Fabrinet may capture repeat manufacturing and test economics even when another company owns the PIC, laser or system architecture. Its current DCI/HPC exposure makes the route relevant, but the filing does not identify CPO. The fixed-price/yield language also means a CPO ramp could create downside through scrap, rework or warranty even if revenue grows. Keep Fabrinet as a manufacturing-route candidate until a named CPO programme, qualified production line, supplier allocation and programme-level margin are documented.

## Required follow-up

- Identify whether any NVIDIA, Broadcom, Coherent or Lumentum CPO programme names Fabrinet as the manufacturing partner.
- Seek customer/SKU-level evidence for optical-engine assembly, fibre attach, test, rework and qualification.
- Separate Fabrinet’s company-wide 12.1% gross margin from any future CPO-engine margin estimate.
