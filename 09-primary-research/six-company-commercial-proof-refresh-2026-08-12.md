## Evidence discipline

The following statements remain explicitly **insufficient** for the commercial
numerator:

- “existing customer traction,” “large-scale commercial deployment,” or
  “co-packaged with custom XPUs” without a named product/customer and accepted
  denominator;
- “beginning production in 2026” without a qualified-output, customer-SKU, or
  shipment record;
- a technology demonstration without a production qualification and service
  record;
- an order value without product mix, quantity, acceptance, repeatability and
  product-margin boundaries; and
- lifecycle labels or technical manuals without customer procurement evidence.

## Decision

The six-company cards remain complete for relative, evidence-gated stances but
not for CPO revenue, EPS, valuation, or profit-pool ranking. The next decisive
records remain:

1. an exact NVIDIA `SN6800-LD`/`SN6810-LD` customer acceptance and repeat event;
2. an exact Broadcom `BCM78919` customer acceptance and repeat event;
3. Lumentum order conversion with product/quantity/margin boundaries;
4. Coherent product allocation and qualified output;
5. TSMC COUPE customer-SKU output and package economics; and
6. Marvell Photonic Fabric named-XPU production and reported revenue/margin.
