## Verdict

The record contains one company-stated CPO production milestone (TSMC), one
family-level named assembly/test route (SPIL), and multiple credible tooling or
capacity signals. It contains **zero** public product-matched records with
stage yield, rework, test throughput/cost and accepted-engine output. **No OSAT or test supplier is eligible** for CPO value-capture attribution.
