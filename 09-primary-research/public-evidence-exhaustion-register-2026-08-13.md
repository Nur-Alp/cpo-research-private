## Indirect-evidence mining protocol

| Route | Search terms / object | Evidence it can legitimately add | Evidence it cannot substitute |
|---|---|---|---|
| Filings, results and transcripts | SKU, CPO, COUPE, ELSFP, optical engine, order, backlog, yield, warranty, customer concentration | Dated order/conversion/capacity/financial boundary | Product margin or customer volume if not specifically disclosed |
| Customer/OEM announcements and manuals | Exact SKU/ordering label, deployed, accepted, production, ports, expansion | Product configuration, acceptance, system denominator, service boundary | Supplier allocation/economics without a linked supplier record |
| Supplier launches/capacity/design wins | Exact SKU plus PIC/EIC/laser/FAU/OSAT/test, qualified, customer, production | Named physical role, qualification or output | Content share, ASP or margin without commercial terms |
| Procurement, certification and standards demos | SKU, supplier, qualification, interoperability, compliance | Configuration and qualification context | Purchase, accepted fleet volume or realised economics |
| Patents, hiring, tool orders and OSAT expansions | Process, attach, test, CPO, customer, line | Capability, process-route and capacity hypothesis | Production output, product allocation or profitability |

## Search completion condition

The project is **as complete as public evidence permits** when every row above
has: (1) a current source-route list, (2) a dated search disposition, (3) an
explicit minimum clearing record, (4) a rerun trigger, and (5) a linked
decision gate. This register records those five elements. Remaining Open fields
then represent market disclosure limits rather than an undefined research task.
