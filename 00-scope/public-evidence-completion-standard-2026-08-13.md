# Public-evidence completion standard

**Status:** Private completion control; not a claim that the investment outcome is known  
**As of:** 13 August 2026

## What “complete” means here

The project is complete **as public information permits** when it has converted
every material unknown into a defined evidence request, searched the relevant
public route, documented the result and connected it to a decision rule. It
does not require the market to disclose customer volumes, supplier economics or
field reliability that companies choose to keep confidential.

## Minimum integrity thresholds

Before any future thesis upgrade, every material new claim must have:

1. a retained primary source or an explicit inference/open-question label;
2. an exact architecture/product/time boundary;
3. a stated non-qualifier and falsification condition;
4. a source-log and claim-ledger entry where it changes a decision; and
5. a destination in the SKU file, company card, architecture scorecard or model gate.

## Final distinction

```text
Research-complete public-evidence system ≠ commercially proven CPO winner
```

The system is allowed to conclude **not established**. It is not allowed to
replace unavailable data with a stronger conclusion, a generic product
announcement, consolidated gross margin or a hidden model assumption.

## Operating outcome

The live decision remains: switch-side 200G CPO is strategically credible and
commercially early; NVIDIA and Broadcom have the clearest product routes; the
public record does not establish accepted-volume leadership, product-linked
supplier profit capture, or a standalone public-equity winner.

The next work is only evidence that can change one of those statements.
