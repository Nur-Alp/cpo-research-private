**Owner:** Nur Alpys  
**Status:** Private release-control matrix; not an investment conclusion  
**As of:** 2026-08-13  
**Purpose:** Test whether the research can answer each eventual final-decision field without replacing the required evidence with a framework, company announcement, or generic technical result.

The [objective completion audit](objective-completion-audit-2026-08-12.md) provides the requirement-by-requirement status across the full research objective. The [public-release manifest](public-release-manifest-2026-08-12.md) is the controlling boundary for any future public render or deployment.

## Operating rule

A dated final conclusion requires direct linked evidence at a shared architecture, product and time boundary. A provisional memo may instead say **no decision / not established**, explain why, and name the next falsification trigger. That does not permit a company ranking, CPO revenue estimate, price target or public claim that the investment question is settled.
